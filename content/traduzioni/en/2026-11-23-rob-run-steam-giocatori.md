There is a free game on Steam pulling in big-budget numbers. **ROB RUN**, an online crime sandbox developed and published by **Kefir**, launched in early access on 4 November and on 17 November reached 549,661 concurrent players, according to SteamDB data. In the 24 hours before this morning, 23 November, the peak was still 532,443 users.

## What ROB RUN is

The Steam page presents it as a brutal crime sandbox populated by thousands of players, where you start at the bottom and try to rise by any means. It is free to download, runs on Windows 10 or later and uses a kernel-level anti-cheat, Easy Anti-Cheat. The developers expect to stay in early access for around 6 to 12 months.

## Why ROB RUN appeals (and why it divides)

Timing matters. ROB RUN took off just as GTA 6 arrived only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and TheGamer describes it as the game PC players left without Rockstar's adventure are turning to.

The reviews, however, are lukewarm. At the time of our check, 46% of the 36,679 English-language reviews were positive, for an overall "Mixed" rating. The AI disclosure on the page also weighs on it: Kefir explains that AI was used to create some graphical elements.
