On **Metacritic**, the ratings war around **GTA 6** has spread to the classics. For a few days now, much-loved games such as **SoulCalibur** for Dreamcast and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time have been receiving floods of zeros and one-line reviews that have nothing to do with the game. The phenomenon was spotted on X between 22 and 23 November and reported by Time Extension, TheGamer and Notebookcheck.

## GTA 6’s Metacritic score: 99 versus 5.7

It all starts from GTA 6’s page. Rockstar’s game, released on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, has a Metascore of 99 from critics. The user score, on the other hand, when we checked it this morning, was 5.7 from over 121,000 ratings on the PS5 page, with 43% negative ratings. As early as 23 November Forbes had flagged the review bombing, with more than 100,000 user ratings in just a few days.

Some of the low scores, according to Notebookcheck, come from Zelda fans: the site cites an analysis published on Reddit according to which many of the users who slammed GTA 6 had rewarded the Ocarina of Time remake released this year.

## The response: zero scores for the classics

At that point some GTA 6 fans reacted by targeting the games it is being compared with. On Metacritic, the titles with the highest Metascore in history are very few, and among them are SoulCalibur and Ocarina of Time. Time Extension also mentions Super Mario Galaxy 2.

On SoulCalibur’s page today the critic score is 98 and the user score 7.7 from around 1,500 ratings. Among the reviews from 24 and 25 November there are zero scores and, conversely, high scores written specifically to balance things out. One says it wants to “counterbalance the GTA review bombing”. The Ocarina of Time remake has also seen its user score drop, as Notebookcheck reports.

## Why review bombing carries little weight

The Metascore is calculated only from critics’ reviews, so user ratings do not affect GTA 6’s 99 or SoulCalibur’s 98. Notebookcheck points out that all you need to vote on Metacritic is an email address.

For anyone who wants to go over the real numbers: the Ocarina remake debuted with [reviews averaging 96 on Metacritic](/articoli/zelda-ocarina-of-time-recensioni/), while GTA 6 took [$3.79 billion on its first day](/articoli/gta-6-vendite-record/).
