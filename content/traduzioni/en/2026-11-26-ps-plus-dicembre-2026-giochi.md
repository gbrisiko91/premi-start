Sony has announced the **PlayStation Plus** monthly games for December 2026: **Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4**, **Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2** and Amnesia: The Bunker. The announcement came yesterday, 25 November, at 17:30 Italian time on the PlayStation Blog. The three titles can be added to your library from Tuesday 1 December to Monday 4 January, with any subscription tier (Essential, Extra or Premium).

## The PS Plus games for December 2026 and their platforms

- Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 (Cross-Gen Edition), for PS5 and PS4
- Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, for PS5 and PS4
- Amnesia: The Bunker, for PS4

Amnesia: The Bunker is listed for PS4 only, but it can also be played on PS5 thanks to backwards compatibility.

## Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4: the remake of the two instalments

The package remakes the third and fourth instalments of the skateboarding series from scratch, in 4K and with the classic two-minute session format. There are also expanded editors for creating skaters and skateparks, and the option to come up with custom goals to share with friends. For anyone who grew up with the PS2-era Tony Hawk games, it is probably the highlight of the month.

## Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 and Amnesia: The Bunker

In Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 you create your own warrior, explore Conton City and play online, including Raid Battles. Amnesia: The Bunker, meanwhile, is a first-person horror game from Frictional Games, the makers of Soma and the previous Amnesia games. It is set in a First World War bunker, where you play French soldier Henri Clément, armed with a revolver and a dynamo torch that makes quite a lot of noise. In its article Kotaku calls it one of the best horror games of recent years.

## Last few days for November’s games

Anyone who has not yet claimed this month’s games has until Monday 30 November. After that date the December titles take their place.

The PlayStation catalogue reaches the end of the year with a very large user base: Sony recently confirmed that [PS5 has passed 100 million consoles sold](/articoli/ps5-100-milioni-console-vendute/).
