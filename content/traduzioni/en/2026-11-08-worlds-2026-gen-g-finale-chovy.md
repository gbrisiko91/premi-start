Seven World Championships, seven eliminations. At his eighth attempt **Chovy** has done it: **Gen.G** beat **Invictus Gaming** 3-0 in the first semi-final of **League of Legends** **Worlds 2026** and claimed the first spot in the final. For the mid laner many consider the best in the world without a world title, it is the first final of his career. Their opponent will emerge tonight from **Bilibili Gaming** against **T1**.

## Chovy's Worlds curse

In Korea, Chovy has won almost everything: in 2026 he claimed the LCK Cup, the Korean league and his fifth season MVP award. At the World Championship, however, he has always fallen short of the final act.

Three semi-finals lost in four years. And his first Worlds, in 2019, had ended against Invictus Gaming, of all teams: seven years later, the same team has opened the door to the final for him.

## Gen.G vs Invictus Gaming 3-0: how it went

It was no contest. Gen.G won game 1 in 34 minutes and game 2 in 29, both on the blue side. Game 3 was the longest, 40 minutes on the red side, but the outcome didn't change.

The standout was **Ruler**: 23 kills, 8 deaths and 33 assists across three games. For Gen.G's ADC it is his first world final since the one he won in 2017 with Samsung Galaxy, nine years ago. On the other side, the best performer was **Rookie**, with 15 kills, but it wasn't enough for IG, who had not reached the World Championship top four since 2019.

Gen.G thus reach the final without having lost a series yet: unbeaten in the Swiss stage, 3-0 against LYON in the quarter-finals and 3-0 against IG in the semi-final.

## Tonight BLG vs T1: the two possible finals

The second semi-final is played tonight in Allen, Texas. Either result makes for a final with a story to tell.

**If T1 go through** it's an all-Korean final, and a rematch of two years ago: in 2024 it was T1 who knocked Gen.G out in the semi-final, 3-1. **Faker** is chasing a fourth consecutive World Championship, a feat no one has ever achieved. Chovy is chasing his first. We told you about it in our [article on the quarter-finals](/articoli/worlds-2026-t1-karmine-semifinali/).

**If Bilibili Gaming go through** it's Korea against China. BLG lost the 2024 world final to T1 and July's MSI final to Hanwha Life, both 3-2. They reach the semi-final after sweeping aside Dplus KIA, with **Knight** in extraordinary form. Beating them would mean closing the season with an MSI-Worlds double for Korea.

## When is the Worlds 2026 final

The final is on **Saturday 14 November** at the **Barclays Center** in Brooklyn, New York, at 20:00 Italian time. It's a best-of-five with Fearless Draft and you can watch it for free on the [LoL Esports Twitch and YouTube channels](https://lolesports.com/). The champions take home 1 million dollars, out of a 5 million prize pool.
