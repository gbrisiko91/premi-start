**Hideki Kamiya**, the creator of Devil May Cry, Bayonetta and Okami, would like to get his hands on **Castlevania**. He said so in the fourteenth episode of "Hideki Kamiya Replies", the series in which he answers fans on his YouTube channel, released this week and picked up by the international press between 25 and 26 November.

In the video Kamiya runs through the publishers he would like to work with in the future. When he gets to **Konami**, he first mentions Getsu Fuma Den, the action series born on the Famicom in 1987, and then Castlevania. "If we made it, it would be incredible, so leave the job to us," is his request, followed immediately by an admission: right now he is a bit busy.

## Kamiya and Castlevania: why it won't happen right away

The commitment he is talking about is the new Okami, announced in 2024 and developed by Clovers, the studio Kamiya founded in 2023 after leaving PlatinumGames, together with Capcom. The game does not have a release date yet. According to ComicBook.com, Kamiya said he will write to the publishers he mentioned only once he has finished the Okami sequel.

Konami has not commented. For now there is only the wish of a creator who has in the past named Castlevania among the games that inspired him most, and no announced project.

## Castlevania in the hands of other studios

The idea is not so far from what Konami is already doing. In 2026, the year of the series' fortieth anniversary, the latest instalment, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, was developed by Motion Twin and Evil Empire, the makers of Dead Cells, rather than by an internal team.

A Castlevania by Kamiya, with his taste for over-the-top action, would be quite a coup. In the meantime, some people are reinventing the saga on their own: a fan has brought [Castlevania: Symphony of the Night to the SNES, with a free demo](/articoli/castlevania-symphony-of-the-night-snes/).
