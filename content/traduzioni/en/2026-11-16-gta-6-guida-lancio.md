**GTA 6 comes out on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** Here is the key information about the launch of **Grand Theft Auto VI**: Italian times, pre-loading, file size, physical copies and the PC version. **Updated: 16 November 2026.**

## When does GTA 6 come out in Italy?

The date announced by Rockstar is **Thursday 19 November 2026**. According to [official Rockstar support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s), **on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** the game is due to unlock at **local midnight on 19 November**: in Italy, during the night between 18 and 19 November.

## GTA 6 preload: date and times

The **digital preload has been available since 10 November**. In Italy, the published information indicates **00:00 on PS5** and **01:00 on Xbox Series X|S**. The early download does not let you play before release.

We have gathered the details in our article on the [GTA 6 preload and Italian times](/articoli/gta-6-preload-peso/).

## How big is GTA 6: 212 or 230 GB?

Rockstar states **up to 212 GB of available space required to pre-load, install and play**, depending on the device, according to the official FAQ. The figure should not be read as the exact download size on each console.

Some **unofficial** photos of physical boxes show **230 GB**: this is not official confirmation of the final size. To be safe, it's worth keeping extra free space for installation and updates.

To understand where the two numbers come from, read our in-depth piece on the [GTA 6 physical copy and the 230 GB](/articoli/gta-6-copia-fisica-230-gb/).

## Is GTA 6 coming out on PC?

**There is no official date for PC.** The platforms announced for 19 November are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PC dates circulating without Rockstar confirmation should not be considered official.

## Physical copies and download codes

Rockstar confirms that the physical boxes available from **12 November** contain a **download code**, not a disc. Keep your receipt and check that the code is valid. We have explained what to do in the [reports about physical copies with a missing code](/articoli/gta-6-copia-fisica-codice-mancante/).

## Official GTA 6 screenshots

Rockstar has published a **Launch Gallery** dedicated to Jason, Lucia and the state of Leonida. You can view the [gallery on the official website](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery) or read our article on the [new GTA 6 screenshots](/articoli/gta-6-nuovi-screenshot/).
