**Skyrim** is **15 years old**. On 11 November 2011 **Bethesda** released **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** on PC, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, and yesterday the studio celebrated with commemorative artwork, deals and new content. But to those hoping for news on **The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel**, Bethesda's answer was clear: nothing for now.

## How Bethesda is celebrating Skyrim's 15th anniversary

The most visible gift is a new illustration by fantasy artist **Karl Fitzgerald**: a Dovahkiin, the Dragonborn, facing a gigantic dragon, posted on the official channels with the hashtag #Skyrim15. On social media Bethesda is also giving away three limited-edition posters and promises a week of community-focused content.

For anyone who wants to head back to Whiterun, there is also:

- **a bundle of five Creations**, the paid mods vetted by Bethesda, including the **Legacy of Orsinium** quest series and the **Dwarven Delves** dungeons, at half the price of the individual items;
- **discounts of up to 80%** on **Skyrim Special Edition** across several platforms;
- Skyrim-themed rewards in **The Elder Scrolls Online**.

## Skyrim 15 years on: a remastered soundtrack is coming

The "remaster" headline that did the rounds yesterday is not about the game: the label **Laced Records** has announced a remastered version of the game's soundtrack, on vinyl and CD, due in 2027. Pre-orders and prices are not yet available.

So no new edition of the game, after a long run of re-releases: the 2016 Special Edition, the Switch and VR versions, through to the 2021 Anniversary Edition. A longevity that has turned Skyrim into a meme ("you can even play it on a fridge") and into one of the best-selling games of all time.

## Elder Scrolls 6 Sentinel: Bethesda denies an announcement

In recent days a podcaster had promised a big announcement "within two days", and many on Reddit had assumed it would be Sentinel, the recently revealed official title of Elder Scrolls 6, as we covered in our [article on The Elder Scrolls 6 Sentinel](/articoli/elder-scrolls-6-sentinel/). The source of the rumour later clarified that Bethesda had nothing to do with it.

It was Bethesda itself that settled the matter, replying to fans: no big announcements, and nothing new on Sentinel "for a while". A cooling of expectations that many took hard, given that the first Elder Scrolls 6 teaser dates back to 2018.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When did Skyrim come out?

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim was released on 11 November 2011 on PC, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360.

### When is The Elder Scrolls 6 Sentinel coming out?

There is no date yet: Bethesda has said it will not share new information on Sentinel for a while.

Not for now: for the 15th anniversary only the remastered soundtrack has been announced, on vinyl and CD, arriving in 2027.
