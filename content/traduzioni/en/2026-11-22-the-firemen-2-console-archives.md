**The Firemen 2: Pete & Danny**, an action game for the original PlayStation released by **Human Entertainment** in 1995, is playable again. Since 19 November 2026 it has been available on PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2 in Hamster's **Console Archives** line, from the same company that has been re-releasing arcade classics for years with Arcade Archives.

The price given by Hamster is $11.99. The publisher points out that the titles on sale vary from country to country, so it is worth checking the Italian PlayStation Store and Nintendo eShop directly.

## The Firemen 2 Pete & Danny, a sequel that stayed in Japan

The first The Firemen came out on the Super Nintendo in 1994. The sequel was made for the Japanese market and once again has the player putting out fires as two firefighters, Pete and Danny.

This re-release also keeps the in-game text in Japanese only. The options and the digital manual, however, are available in several languages, which at least helps you find your way around the menus.

## What the Console Archives version adds

According to Hamster's press release, the new edition lets you customise the button layout, enable rapid fire and use multiple save points. There are also screen settings and filters that mimic the look of old cathode-ray tube televisions.
