**World of Warcraft: Forever** has broken every record. A few days after its 4 November debut, **Blizzard** announced on social media that it is the biggest launch in the history of **World of Warcraft**: the peak of concurrent players exceeds that of landmark expansions such as **Wrath of the Lich King**, and no launch had ever brought so many former subscribers back into the game.

Blizzard has not released precise figures, but the signal is clear: the return to the original Azeroth is working.

## What is World of Warcraft Forever

It is neither a new expansion for modern WoW nor a simple re-release of **WoW Classic**. Forever is a permanent, reimagined version of the original Azeroth, set before the events of the 2004 game: you level from 1 to 60 across the **Eastern Kingdoms** and **Kalimdor**, with plenty of new features.

According to the [official launch post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315820), the main additions are:

- over **1,000 new quests**, nine new five-player dungeons and three level-60 raids, arriving on 9 December;
- new or reworked zones, such as **Mount Hyjal** and the Riverglades;
- the new playable **Skyborne** race and new race and class combinations;
- the choice between a "Classic" and an "Enhanced" experience, plus a camp system and account-wide progression.

A regular World of Warcraft subscription is all you need to play. Unlocking the Skyborne, however, requires buying a separate pack.

## Why the WoW Forever launch is a record

The most striking figure is the number of **returning players**: according to Blizzard, there have never been so many in more than twenty years of the game's history. It confirms a trend already seen during the beta, the biggest the studio has ever run, with almost nine million characters created.

The tech also played its part: players have described it as the smoothest launch in WoW history, with queues of just a few minutes, little lag and rare disconnections. According to Windows Central, the credit goes to the **megaserver** architecture, which can handle many more players in the same space than the old realms. Vice president Holly Longdale commented on it wryly, calling it a "so far so goodish" start.

Not everything is perfect: Blizzard has already said it wants to make the capital cities livelier, as some find them empty precisely because players are spread across multiple copies of the cities.

## What to expect from WoW Forever in the coming weeks

The next milestone is **9 December**, when the three endgame raids open, including a new version of Onyxia's Lair. Later on, the **Hardcore** mode will also arrive, with a single life per character.

For Microsoft, which owns Blizzard, this is excellent news at a time of major upheaval among the Xbox studios. Among the group's most anticipated returns is also **The Elder Scrolls VI**, which we covered in our article on [The Elder Scrolls 6 Sentinel](/articoli/elder-scrolls-6-sentinel/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is World of Warcraft Forever free?

No. WoW Forever is included in the World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. The Skyborne packs, with the new race, are purchased separately.

### When was World of Warcraft Forever released?

It launched worldwide on 4 November 2026 at 15:00 Pacific time, which is midnight between 4 and 5 November in Italy.

### What's the difference between WoW Classic and WoW Forever?

WoW Classic brings back the original game exactly as it was. Forever starts from the same era but adds zones, quests, dungeons and a new race, and it is designed to remain permanent.
