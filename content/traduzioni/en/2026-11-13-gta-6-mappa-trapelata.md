Six days before launch, the **GTA 6 map** has made its way online. On 12 November, photos of the printed map of **Leonida** included in the official Collector's Box, taken at a Selfridges store in the UK, started circulating on social media. **Rockstar Games** has not commented and has not shown the map officially, so it should be treated as a leak, albeit a very credible one. Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Where the leaked GTA 6 map comes from

According to GamesRadar and tbreak, the first photo was posted on X by a user who saw the Collector's Box on display in a Selfridges store in Manchester, while the sharpest image came from an Instagram account that was later deleted. The art style matches the promotional images of the box, which includes a double-sided souvenir poster: in all likelihood, that is exactly what this is.

The [Rockstar Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/merchandise/gtavi-goodtime-state-vice-city-collection) confirms a **double-sided Leonida map souvenir poster** inside the Goodtime State – Vice City Collection. The set costs **$399.99 in the US** and **does not include the game**. The identity of whoever shared the full image is not confirmed.

## The Leonida locations revealed by the leak

This is a **stylised souvenir poster**, not a technical map of the game. Reports on the photos identify possible names such as **Goose Key, Lummox County, Southwind and Pelican Valley**, though some labels are partly obscured. **Rockstar has not verified these names, nor any counts of counties, islands or towns.**

The general motorway layout is also visible, though not the full road network. And the most eagle-eyed fans have already spotted a UFO drawn above a quarry: it could be a nod to GTA 5's alien easter eggs, but that remains to be seen.

## GTA 6 nearly rejected in South Korea over nudity

This is a separate story from the map leak. According to [Inven Global's report on the original GCRB minutes](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/28517/gta-6-came-close-to-refused-classification-in-south-korea), South Korea's rating board debated out-of-context nudity. The review ended in an adults-only rating on 16 October, not a ban.

There were three reviews, between September and October: the first ended in a tie, then on 16 October the game received an adults-only rating with eight votes in favour and one against. Those who played it through concluded that the scenes with nudity are not sexual and that the sex scenes do not show genitals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the leaked GTA 6 map real?

Rockstar confirms that the Collector's Box includes a double-sided Leonida poster, **but has not authenticated the complete image circulating online**.

### When does GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; preloading has been open since 10 November.

### How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published official figures for the world's size or its number of counties, islands and towns. The souvenir poster cannot establish those measurements.
