The **Hyperkin Mega95** is about to become reality. The American maker of retro accessories has announced that its handheld **Mega Drive**, which can play original cartridges, has completed development and testing and has entered **mass production**. Pre-order details, the company promises, will arrive soon.

## Hyperkin Mega95: what the company announced

The update came in an official **Hyperkin** post on X, picked up in recent days by Time Extension and Everyeye. According to the company, the research and development phase and testing with the community are complete, production has started and the packaging is being finalised.

The video accompanying the announcement shows the console both in handheld mode and connected to a TV via the dock. Still missing, however, is the information everyone is waiting for: **price** and **release date** have not yet been announced.

## Mega95 specs: screen, dock and cartridges

The Mega95 was unveiled in January 2024 with a simple idea: a **Sega Mega Drive** (Genesis in the United States) to take on the go, using real cartridges instead of the preloaded ROMs of mini consoles. The features announced at the time:

- a **5-inch** widescreen display, with a switch between 4:3 and 16:9;
- controls inspired by the Mega Drive's **six-button** pad;
- a USB-C **dock** with HDMI output and two ports for original controllers.

Hyperkin hasn't yet said whether these specs have remained unchanged in the final version: we'll find out when pre-orders open. There are also no official figures yet on the battery and battery life.

## Why the handheld Mega Drive took so long

It has been a long road. After the 2024 announcement, the launch slipped several times due to production problems. In early 2026 Hyperkin publicly explained the delay, saying it preferred to arrive late rather than sell a product that wasn't up to collectors' standards.

The name is a tribute to the **Nomad**, the handheld Mega Drive that Sega launched in 1995 only in North America. For anyone in Italy who still has their cartridge collection, the Mega95 could be a convenient way to replay them without emulation. And interest in the Sega console remains sky-high, as shown by the [Mega Drive prototypes recovered from a former Sega developer](/articoli/prototipi-mega-drive-sega-sti/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the Hyperkin Mega95 come out?

There's no date yet. Hyperkin has confirmed that mass production has begun and that pre-order information will arrive shortly.

### How much does the Mega95 cost?

The price hasn't been announced. Hyperkin will reveal it when pre-orders open.

### Does the Mega95 work with European Mega Drive cartridges?

Hyperkin talks about compatibility with Genesis and Mega Drive cartridges, but hasn't clarified support for 50 Hz PAL games. It's best to wait for the final specs.
