The delay of **God of War Laufey** is official. **Santa Monica Studio** announced yesterday, 4 November, that the new chapter in the saga will arrive on **PlayStation 5** on **20 May 2027**, instead of 16 February. That's three extra months, which the studio will use for final polish.

## God of War Laufey delayed: the new release date

The announcement came in a message posted on Santa Monica Studio's official social channels. The reason is the classic one for last-mile delays: the team wants to complete the polishing phase and bring the game up to its full potential, without going into technical details.

The studio thanked players for their patience and promised new updates soon. There's no indication of changes to the editions already available for pre-order: Standard, **Digital Deluxe** and the Deluxe upgrade are still listed on the official PlayStation.com page.

## Why God of War Laufey is so eagerly awaited

**God of War Laufey** was unveiled in June and is the first main instalment in the series in which you don't control **Kratos**. The protagonist is **Faye**, known as Laufey the Just: Kratos's wife and **Atreus**'s mother, whose death opens 2018's **God of War**.

According to the official description, Faye awakens in the gods' afterlife after her own funeral and must find a way back to her family. The realm she moves through is contested by deities from different mythologies, and combat focuses on a new weapon and on the magic of the giants, the **Jötnar**. The February date had been revealed in July, during San Diego Comic-Con.

## What changes for the 2027 PS5 line-up

With the move to late May, the first quarter of 2027 loses its most important PlayStation title. Spring, after all, is a period that has served the studio well: 2018's God of War came out on 20 April.

It's not the only big game to slip in recent weeks: FromSoftware has also taken more time, as we reported when covering [The Duskbloods' delay to 2027](/articoli/the-duskbloods-rinvio-2027/). For God of War Laufey, the question now is whether Santa Monica will show new gameplay as early as **The Game Awards** in December.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does God of War Laufey come out?

God of War Laufey comes out on 20 May 2027 on PlayStation 5. The previous date was 16 February 2027.

### Why was God of War Laufey delayed?

Santa Monica Studio explained that it needs more time for final polish and to bring the game up to its full potential.

### Will God of War Laufey come out on PC?

For now it has only been announced for PS5. Sony has brought previous instalments to PC, but there's no confirmation yet for Laufey.
