A purple, boxy, trapezoid-shaped **PlayStation 2**. A silver **DualShock** with what is probably a built-in microphone. These are some of the never-before-seen prototypes emerging from **PlayStation: The First 30 Years**, the celebratory volume produced by **Read-Only Memory** together with **Sony**, which has started shipping in recent days. The first pages to circulate online are doing the rounds in the retro community.

## What's in PlayStation: The First 30 Years

The book is a brick in the literal sense: **400 pages**, a 37.5 x 29.5 cm format and a weight of about five kilos. It collects photographs of console prototypes, experimental controllers, sketches, resin models and industrial design studies, from the first **PlayStation** in 1994 all the way to the **PS5**.

The story is told by three key names in Sony hardware: **Ken Kutaragi**, regarded as the father of the PlayStation, designer **Teiyu Goto**, who was behind the first console and the original controller, and **Yujin Morisawa**, who designed the PS5. The photos are by Benedict Redgrove, with art direction by Michael C Place.

## The trapezoid PS2 and Sony's other prototypes

Among the most shared images is an early PS2 prototype: a more cubic body, a tall front plate, long air vents on the sides and a dark purple colour. Nothing like the black, rectangular monolith with a front tray that reached shops in 2000 and became the best-selling console of all time.

Also sparking discussion is a controller concept, probably from the **PS3** era: silver, with the PS button, shapes halfway between curves and sharp edges, and what looks like a microphone. Time Extension bluntly describes it as unwieldy compared with the DualShock 3 we know. The book also includes the famous "boomerang" controller shown at the time of the PS3's unveiling.

For lovers of this kind of video game archaeology, it has the same appeal as the [Mega Drive prototypes resurfaced from SEGA's archives](/articoli/prototipi-mega-drive-sega-sti/): seeing the paths manufacturers discarded helps explain why consoles became the way we remember them.

## How much the PlayStation book costs and where to buy it

It can only be bought from the [official Read-Only Memory website](https://readonlymemory.com/pages/playstation-the-first-30-years). The standard edition costs **£95** (about €111 according to Time Extension), with an embossed slipcase, metallic lettering and four fabric ribbon bookmarks. The £245 Deluxe edition, limited to 1,994 copies, has already sold out.

Shipping was originally planned for spring 2026 and slipped by a few months to complete production and transport: the official page now states that the book is being delivered.
