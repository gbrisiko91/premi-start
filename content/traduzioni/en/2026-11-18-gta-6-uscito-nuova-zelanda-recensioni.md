**GTA 6** has begun its journey around the world. At midnight on 19 November in New Zealand, 12:00 on Wednesday 18th in Italy, **Grand Theft Auto VI** unlocked on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the reviews went live at the same moment. The press verdict is almost unanimous: 99 on Metacritic and 99 on OpenCritic.

## GTA 6 review scores

According to Tom's Guide's live blog, OpenCritic showed an average of 99 at 11:06 UTC, with 100% of critics recommending the game. Metacritic reached 99 a few minutes later; Multiplayer.it reports more than 120 reviews already counted.

10s and 100s are the norm. Among international outlets, IGN, GameSpot, Game Informer, Eurogamer, VGC, Edge, GamesRadar+, Push Square, Destructoid and The Washington Post gave it top marks. The lowest scores seen so far are still extremely high: 96 from Wccftech and 90 from the New York Times and TheSixthAxis.

IGN's Luke Reilly calls it "the most impressive video game I've ever played". For VGC it is "a modern masterpiece".

10s are raining down in Italy too. According to ItaliaTopGames' round-up, Multiplayer.it (review by Pierpaolo Greco), Everyeye, SpazioGames and Gamesurf all awarded full marks.

## GTA 6 in New Zealand: what is happening online

The launch is rolling out by time zone: according to Rockstar, the game unlocks at local midnight in each country. New Zealand is first, followed by Australia, Japan and then gradually Europe.

In the last few hours the first clips have started circulating on social media. The most shared, posted by the X account @gtasix_, shows an alleged loading screen with two pink flamingos, but Rockstar has not confirmed it is authentic. Anyone who wants to reach Vice City without spoilers would do well to stay away from X, TikTok, YouTube and Twitch until tonight.

Some are also trying to play early with the so-called New Zealand trick. On Xbox, Kotaku explains, you just set New Zealand as the console's region and restart. On PS5, however, you need a new New Zealand PSN account and to buy the game from the local PlayStation Store. It is not a risk-free shortcut: according to Notebookcheck, a Twitch partner manager reportedly warned German creator Rohat of a possible suspension if he streamed the game before its release in his country.

## When you can play GTA 6 in Italy

In Italy the unlock is scheduled for midnight between Wednesday 18 and Thursday 19 November, as stated by Rockstar's official support. The game is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: a PC version has not been announced.
