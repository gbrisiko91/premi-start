**Thief: The Dark Project Remastered** has a release date: **3 December 2026**. **Nightdive Studios**, **Eidos-Montréal** and **Atari** announced it, also opening pre-orders for the physical editions. **Looking Glass Studios**' 1998 stealth classic thus returns to PC and consoles 28 years later, bringing with it a brand-new campaign.

## When Thief Remastered comes out and on which platforms

The release is set for 3 December, as also confirmed by the official Steam page. The game will arrive on:

- **PC** via Steam, Epic Games Store and GOG;
- **PS5** and PS4;
- **Xbox Series X|S**, with Xbox Play Anywhere support;
- **Nintendo Switch 2** and Switch, with a free upgrade to the Switch 2 version for those starting on the original console.

The price is **€29.99** for the standard edition and €34.99 for the Digital Deluxe, which adds the soundtrack, an illustrated development journal and an excerpt from an upcoming novel. For PS5 and Switch 2 there are also physical editions distributed by Clear River Games, including one with a steelbook and a Deluxe that recreates the original's trapezoidal box, an item collectors will be eyeing up.

## What's new in Thief: The Dark Project Remastered

Nightdive, by now a specialist in bringing back classics such as *System Shock* and *Quake*, has refreshed the game with its **KEX engine**. The remaster includes all of *Thief Gold*, the 1999 re-release with three extra missions and five new enemy types, for a total of 15 missions with three difficulty levels each.

Among the technical improvements:

- resolution up to 4K and 120 frames per second, with improved textures, models and animations;
- full controller support, with rumble, gyro aiming and a wheel for weapons and items;
- a selector to replay completed missions;
- achievements and trophies;
- a Vault section with unseen behind-the-scenes material;
- support for fan-made campaigns.

## The Tarnished Mirror, the new campaign

The biggest surprise is **The Tarnished Mirror**, a brand-new four-mission campaign included at launch. It is made by the team behind **The Black Parade**, one of the most acclaimed mods in the Thief community, and is set between the first game and *Thief II: The Metal Age*. The thief Garrett ends up in prison after a heist gone wrong and has to deal with uneasy alliances and a sunken necropolis with Lovecraftian atmosphere.

It is a fine recognition for a community that has kept the game alive for almost thirty years. And an example of how the history of '90s games continues to fascinate, as the recent [row between Romero, Petersen and Carmack over the origins of DOOM's Deathmatch](/articoli/doom-deathmatch-romero-petersen-carmack/) also shows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Thief: The Dark Project Remastered come out?

On 3 December 2026 on PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and Switch.

### How much does Thief Remastered cost?

€29.99 for the standard edition and €34.99 for the Digital Deluxe. The physical editions for PS5 and Switch 2 are priced differently.

### Is Thief Remastered a remake?

No, it is a remaster: the original 1998 game with improved graphics, modern controls and a new campaign, not a rebuild from scratch.
