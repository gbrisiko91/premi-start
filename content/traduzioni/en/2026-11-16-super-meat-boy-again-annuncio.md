**Team Meat** has announced **Super Meat Boy Again+**, a remake of the platformer that drove players mad (and made them die thousands of times) in 2010. The game arrived on **Steam** with a store page and a trailer on 13 November. For now it is planned only for PC and the release date is still to be announced.

## What Super Meat Boy Again+ is

The studio presents it as a new version of the first Super Meat Boy, with remade visuals, music, cutscenes, levels, secrets and unlockables. The tagline on the Steam page is "Everything is new but familiar".

These are the features listed by the studio:

- more than 300 levels;
- a soundtrack by Danny Baranowsky, aka Danny B, the composer of the original;
- animated cutscenes;
- a new visual and audio style;
- new and old secrets;
- circular saws, which appear in the list as an item in their own right.

Team Meat hasn't given any further details on the game's structure. We don't know how many levels are taken from the original and how many are brand new.

## Will Super Meat Boy Again+ be in Italian?

The Steam page lists ten languages, including Italian, but only for the interface: no Italian subtitles or audio. In a platformer with little text it makes little difference, but the cutscenes might stay in English.

The minimum requirements are low: 64-bit Windows 10, 4 GB of RAM, 3 GB of storage and a processor and graphics card from after 2015. Achievements, leaderboards and Steam Cloud saves are planned.

## When it comes out and how much it costs

Team Meat has announced neither the date nor the price, and hasn't mentioned console versions. Anyone interested can add it to their Steam wishlist.
