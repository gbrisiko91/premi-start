**Zelda Ocarina of Time** remake **sales** are record-breaking: **Nintendo** announced on 11 November that the game has passed **5.2 million copies** worldwide in the first four days after its 5 November release. No other game published by Nintendo on **Switch 2** had got off to such a strong start.

## Ocarina of Time sales: the figures from Nintendo's press release

[Nintendo's official press release](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/release/en/2026/261111.html) cites more than 5.2 million units across physical and digital copies, of which **810,000 in Japan**. For the Kyoto company it is the best launch ever for one of its own games on Switch 2, measured over the first four days.

The result follows a near-perfect critical reception: as we reported, the remake debuted with [96 on Metacritic, the highest score of 2026](/articoli/zelda-ocarina-of-time-recensioni/). A combination of review scores and nostalgia that has clearly also won over those who had never played the 1998 **Nintendo 64** classic.

## How it compares with Mario Kart World and Pokopia

According to GamesRadar+'s calculations, Ocarina of Time sold more than twice as much in four days as **Pokémon Pokopia**, which had stopped at 2.2 million over the same period. In four days it has also already overtaken the total for **Donkey Kong Bananza**, which stood at 5.05 million at the end of September.

Only **Mario Kart World**, the console's launch game, remains ahead: 5.63 million in its first month and 16.48 million by the end of September, though helped by console bundles. In the United Kingdom, according to figures reported by British outlets, the remake is already the biggest physical launch of 2026 and the second-best debut ever for a Zelda game, by a wide margin over **Breath of the Wild**.

## What it means for Nintendo and for remakes

The success confirms a trend: great classics rebuilt from scratch sell as well as, if not better than, many new games. And it comes at a time when the series is celebrating its 40th anniversary, with Nintendo having already announced additional content such as [Master Quest coming as DLC](/articoli/ocarina-of-time-master-quest-dlc/).

The next official figure will come with the February quarterly results, which will show whether Ocarina of Time can really challenge Mario Kart World. One thing is clear, though: for many players this is the reason to buy a Switch 2 for Christmas.
