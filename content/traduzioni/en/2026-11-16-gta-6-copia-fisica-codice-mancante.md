Some buyers of the **GTA 6 physical copy** say they opened the box and didn't find a valid code: in some cases the strip is blank, in others the code shows as already redeemed. The reports appeared on Reddit and X in the days after 12 November, when the boxes reached shops, and were picked up by Kotaku and Notebookcheck. **Rockstar Games** has not yet made any statement. So far we have found no reported cases in Italy.

The problem matters more than usual because the physical editions of **Grand Theft Auto VI** don't contain a disc. Inside the box there is only a code to download the game, which comes out on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Anyone who finds a blank strip has no other way to install the game.

## What players with a GTA 6 physical copy are reporting

According to Kotaku, the code is printed on a grey patterned strip inside the cover. It looks like a scratch-off label, but the code is printed on top of it. Some photos posted online show the strip with no code at all.

Notebookcheck reports three concrete cases:

- an X user showed the inside cover of a PS5 copy with the grey strip blank, and Best Buy reportedly refused them a refund;
- a Reddit user who had pre-ordered directly from Rockstar received the bonus poster but no PlayStation Store code, and two support tickets were reportedly closed without a solution;
- the X account GTA 6 Report has collected other similar reports.

The issue, again according to Notebookcheck, doesn't seem widespread and affects both PlayStation and Xbox. Neither outlet gives a number of cases.

## Why it's happening: the theories

There is no official explanation. Notebookcheck thinks a manufacturing error before the copies reached shops is most likely. Kotaku recalls a precedent with Batman: Arkham City, when some copies shipped without the code for a DLC.

The "torch trick" is also doing the rounds online: shining a strong light on the side of the box would supposedly reveal the code without opening it. Kotaku's editor-in-chief tried it on his own copy without success. Notebookcheck, however, notes that some European boxes lack an extra protective layer, which raises fears that someone could read and steal codes before sale. It is a suspicion: there is no evidence that this has happened.

## What to do if your copy of GTA 6 has no code

In its support FAQ, Rockstar says that for purchases made at other shops you need to contact the retailer. The PlayStation support page dedicated to GTA 6 codes says the same for damaged or missing codes. So it's worth photographing the box and the code strip straight away, keeping your receipt and contacting the shop as soon as possible. For physical copies bought from the Rockstar Store, on the other hand, the FAQ points to Xsolla support, which handles Rockstar store purchases.
