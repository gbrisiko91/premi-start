There is a new official game tied to **Fallout**, and its stars are cats: it is called **A Wasteland Full of Cats** and it is being developed by Brazilian studio **Devcats**. The project appeared in the database of the Australian classification board, and in recent days Bethesda confirmed it through Fallout's official channels, describing it as a "Fallout-inspired" adventure.

According to Game Rant, the series' official account admitted that the game came out earlier than planned and promised more details soon. For now there are no images, trailer, platforms, price or release date.

## A Wasteland Full of Cats in the Australian classification

The Australian Classification Board listing is the most concrete source we have. The game received a PG rating, with a warning for mild themes and violence, and is listed as produced in 2026 in Brazil. Devcats appears as both developer and publisher, while the classification application was submitted by ZeniMax Australia, the subsidiary of the group that owns Bethesda.

The listing's official description says the game is made "in collaboration with Bethesda and the Fallout franchise". It mentions 14 settings and 33 rooms to explore, a Vault with levels that fans of the series will recognise, and a final showdown in which you shoot radioactive cockroaches, the radroaches every Fallout player has met at least once.

## Fallout cat game: what is still missing

The 14 settings and 33 rooms suggest a modestly sized project, a far cry from the open-world role-playing games of the main series. Bethesda will tell us the rest: platforms, price and release window have not yet been announced.
