**Sea of Sorrow**, the first major expansion for **Hollow Knight: Silksong**, will be released on 11 December 2026 and will be free for all players. **Team Cherry** announced it on Friday 27 November during Cherry Time, a special presentation, together with a new trailer.

On its official blog the Australian studio writes that the content arrives "free for all players on December 11th". Anyone who already owns Silksong will not have to buy anything.

## What we know about Sea of Sorrow

Team Cherry says that in Sea of Sorrow players will travel above and below the forgotten ocean of Pharloom, among islands, fortresses and sunken caverns. The blog promises crazy characters and epic bosses, as well as new music by Christopher Larkin, the composer of both games.

The trailer, as described by GamesRadar+, shows Hornet arriving by boat, fighting pirate bugs, getting fired out of a cannon and climbing ship rigging. The studio presents it as an expansion unlike any it has made so far. Length, number of bosses and new abilities have not yet been announced.

## A date a year in the waiting

Sea of Sorrow had been announced as a free, sea-themed expansion with a release planned for 2026, but until now it had no date. In early November the physical edition's manual mentioned further free packs after this one, as we reported in our article on [Silksong and the free DLC revealed by the manual](/articoli/silksong-sea-of-sorrow-dlc-gratis/). Now we know the first will arrive before the end of the year. Team Cherry has not yet said what the following content will be.

## The other news from Cherry Time

In the same presentation Team Cherry showed the games from its new label Team Cherry Presents, developed by friends of the studio, including Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City and Radio Rain. They do not have a date yet: the studio invites players to add them to their Steam wishlist. The blog also announces that Huy Le has joined as the fourth member of the team, in the role of technical director for these projects.
