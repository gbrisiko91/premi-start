The **Xbox Nano Controller**, the smallest controller **Microsoft** has ever made, hits shops this week, from 4 November according to several outlets. In Italy it costs **€49.99**. And just days before launch a surprise has emerged: a feature called **Cloud connect** that lets it connect directly to **Xbox Cloud Gaming** over Wi-Fi, just like the **Google Stadia** controller did.

## Xbox Nano Controller: price and release in Italy

Microsoft unveiled the Nano Controller on 14 October on Xbox Wire, opening pre-orders straight away. The official price is $49.99 in the United States, and in Italy it is offered at €49.99. Italy is among the countries where it arrives from the November launch, along with most of Europe, the United States, Japan and Brazil.

It therefore costs less than a standard Xbox wireless controller, and it targets a specific audience: people with small hands, children, travellers and those who play in the cloud on a phone, tablet or TV.

## How small is the new Xbox controller

The measurements explain the name: it's about **12 centimetres** long and weighs around **100 grams**, less than most modern smartphones. Despite this, it keeps all **18 inputs** of a classic Xbox controller with the traditional layout.

Other features confirmed by Microsoft:

- **built-in rechargeable battery**, no AA batteries (battery life hasn't been announced);
- **Xbox Wireless** and **Bluetooth** connectivity, for Xbox consoles, PC, smartphones and tablets;
- a **programmable Action button**, for example for screenshots and recordings;
- also designed for local multiplayer, with Microsoft citing **Minecraft Dungeons II** and **Forza Horizon 6**.

## Cloud connect, the "Stadia-style" feature

The novelty that got people talking most wasn't in the announcement. As discovered by The Verge and reported by 9to5Google, **Cloud connect** appears in the settings: the controller can connect by itself to the Wi-Fi network and to Xbox Cloud Gaming servers, without going through the Bluetooth of the device you're watching the game on.

It's the same trick as the Stadia controller, which reduced latency because it talked directly to the cloud. Microsoft, however, presents it as a **preview still in development**, which could change: it's not clear when it will be available to everyone or which devices it will work on.

## Why people are talking about it

The Nano arrives at a time when Microsoft is pushing hard on multi-screen gaming, from smart TVs to headsets. In the United States some Samsung TVs and Meta Quest headsets will include it in bundles with two months of **Game Pass Ultimate**. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does the Xbox Nano Controller cost in Italy?

The price given in Italy is €49.99, the same number as the American price in dollars.

### Does the Xbox Nano Controller work on PC and smartphones?

Yes. It connects via Xbox Wireless and Bluetooth to Xbox consoles, Windows PCs, phones and tablets.

### What is Cloud connect on the Xbox Nano?

It's a preview feature that connects the controller directly to Xbox Cloud Gaming via Wi-Fi. Microsoft says it is still in development and could change.
