The **GTA 6 preload** will begin on **10 November**, nine days before the 19 November release. **Rockstar Games** confirmed it on its Newswire: anyone who has bought the digital version on **PS5** or **Xbox Series X|S** will be able to download the game in advance. The figure causing the most discussion is the file size: around **212 GB**.

## When the GTA 6 preload starts in Italy

The times vary depending on the console:

- **PlayStation 5**: the preload opens at local midnight on 10 November, so in Italy at 00:00 between 9 and 10 November. The system follows your account's time zone, and as always players in New Zealand will be the first to start.
- **Xbox Series X|S**: it starts at the same moment worldwide, at 00:00 GMT on 10 November. In Italy, on winter time, that means **01:00** on 10 November.

Compared with expectations, it is a gift for those who pre-ordered digitally: until recently it was thought that the early download would start on 12 November, a week before launch.

## GTA 6 and physical copies with a code in the box

Those who chose a physical edition with a code in the box will have to wait instead: these versions will reach shops from **12 November**, and only from then will it be possible to redeem the code and start downloading. Even if your usual shop hands you your copy early, it is still worth freeing up space on your SSD in good time.

On release day, 19 November, the game will unlock on PS5 at local midnight. Rockstar has not yet clarified the exact unlock time on Xbox.

## How big is GTA 6: 212 GB on your SSD

The stated size is **212 GB**, close to the estimates of around 210 GB that circulated in recent weeks. For comparison, on a 512 GB **Xbox Series S**, which leaves around 364 GB free, GTA 6 alone would take up more than half the space: many will have to clear things out.

Bear in mind too that a day-one patch could add more gigabytes to the total. The advice is to free up some extra margin beyond the 212 GB, especially if you have a launch-model PS5, with around 667 GB of usable space.

## Why the GTA 6 preload is so eagerly awaited

Grand Theft Auto VI is the most anticipated game of the decade, and its arrival has pushed several publishers to move their releases away from November. In its announcement, **Rockstar** reminds players there is still time to pre-order, while social media is full of players getting organised for the nights of 9 and 18 November.

The game is console-only: no PC version has been announced for now. Staying on the subject of big releases that have been delayed or are eagerly awaited, it is also worth recalling the recent [delay of The Duskbloods to 2027](/articoli/the-duskbloods-rinvio-2027/).
