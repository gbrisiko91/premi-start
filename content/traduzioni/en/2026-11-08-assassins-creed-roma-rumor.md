**Assassin's Creed** could be heading back to **Rome**, ancient Rome this time. According to an unconfirmed rumour shared on 7 November by French content creator **J0nathan**, **Ubisoft** has reportedly greenlit two new games in the series: one, known internally as **Mars**, would be set in ancient Rome; the other, **Pocket**, would be a smartphone title set in Jerusalem.

Ubisoft has not commented. The rumour has been picked up by several Italian and international outlets, but none has been able to fully verify it.

## What the Assassin's Creed Mars rumour says

J0nathan, who has had contacts inside Ubisoft in the past, claims to have two sources with access to the company's internal systems. Both reportedly confirmed the existence of the two projects:

- **Assassin's Creed Mars**: an instalment set in ancient Rome, with no known platforms, historical period or development studio;
- **Assassin's Creed Pocket**: a game for iOS and Android set in Jerusalem, the city of the first Assassin's Creed.

The codename is no coincidence: **Mars** was the Roman god of war. According to J0nathan, the choice of setting could be linked to the Assassin's Creed TV series in the works at Netflix, which has long been rumoured to take place in Roman antiquity.

## How reliable is the Assassin's Creed in Rome rumour

Caution is needed here. **Tom Henderson** of Insider Gaming, one of the most closely followed journalists on Ubisoft leaks, said he had already heard the names Mars and Pocket from his sources, but could not confirm the new claims to his own standards. The reason, he explains, is that the management of the series has changed a great deal recently.

In other words: the codenames seem to exist, while the details about the settings remain to be verified. And a project approved today can change shape, or disappear, before it reaches the shelves.

## When could an Assassin's Creed in ancient Rome come out

If the project exists, we won't see it any time soon. The next major instalment is **Assassin's Creed Hexe**, set in the Holy Roman Empire during the witch hunts: according to Insider Gaming it has reportedly run into development problems and slipped internally by a year, probably to 2028. A game set in Rome would therefore come later, towards the end of the decade.

For Italian fans it would be a homecoming of sorts: Rome already took centre stage in **Assassin's Creed Brotherhood** in 2010, but in its Renaissance version, with Ezio Auditore. Roman antiquity has for years been one of the settings the community asks for most, which is also one of the reasons the rumour spread so quickly.

Meanwhile, Ubisoft is also making moves outside the series: according to Bloomberg it has made an offer for the Dishonored studio, as we reported in our article on [Ubisoft and the purchase of Arkane Lyon](/articoli/arkane-lyon-ubisoft/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Assassin's Creed in Rome confirmed?

No. It is a rumour shared by J0nathan and not verified by Insider Gaming. Ubisoft has announced nothing.

### What is the next Assassin's Creed release?

The next major instalment expected is Assassin's Creed Hexe, set in central Europe during the witch hunts. The date is not official: rumours point to 2028.

### Is there already an Assassin's Creed set in Rome?

Yes, Assassin's Creed Brotherhood (2010) is set in Renaissance Rome. No main instalment has so far taken place in ancient Rome, although Assassin's Creed Origins shows that era in Egypt.
