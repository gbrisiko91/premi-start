**PlayStation exclusives** are proudly showing themselves again. The official pages for **Ghost of Yotei** and **Until Dawn 2** on playstation.com now carry the label **PlayStation Exclusive**, spotted over the weekend by a user on X and picked up by Push Square and Multiplayer.it. For many, it is the clearest sign yet that **Sony** is going back to betting on games that can only be played on **PS5**.

## Where the PlayStation Exclusive branding appears

The logo is visible next to the title on the official pages of both games. On the page for Until Dawn 2, due on 28 January 2027, the FAQ also confirms that the game is exclusive to PS5. The first to point it out was the user Radec on X, known for closely tracking changes to the store.

Sony did not accompany the change with a press release, so we do not know whether the branding will appear on every game from its in-house studios. It is easy to imagine it, though, on the upcoming big titles such as **God of War Laufey**, whose [delay](/articoli/god-of-war-laufey-rinvio/) we covered, and **Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet** from **Naughty Dog**.

## Why Sony is betting on PS5 exclusives again

In recent years Sony has brought many of its most famous games to PC, from **God of War** to **Marvel's Spider-Man** and **The Last of Us**, often a couple of years after their console release. According to several outlets, however, the results on Steam reportedly fell short of expectations, and there has been talk for some time of an internal change of course in favour of exclusivity, at least for the big single-player adventures.

The exception remains multiplayer and "live service" games, which Sony will continue to release on PC as well: the nearest example is **Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls**. A two-speed strategy, then, that is more reminiscent of the PlayStation of old than that of recent years.

## What changes for PC and PS5 players

For console players the message is simple: Sony's big games will give one more reason to buy a PS5, or the future PS6. Those who play only on PC, on the other hand, may have to wait much longer, or never see some titles at all. It remains to be seen whether the branding signals a fixed rule or just a different way of presenting the games: for now it is a clue, not an announcement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will Ghost of Yotei come out on PC?

Not at the moment: the official page presents it as a PlayStation exclusive and Sony has not announced a PC version.

### Is Until Dawn 2 a PS5 exclusive?

Yes, according to the official playstation.com page the game is exclusive to PS5 and comes out on 28 January 2027.

### Will Sony stop bringing its games to PC?

There is no official announcement. The signs suggest that single-player games will stay on PS5 for longer, while multiplayer games will continue to arrive on PC as well.
