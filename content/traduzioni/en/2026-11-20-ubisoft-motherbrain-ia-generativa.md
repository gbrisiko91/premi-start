**Ubisoft** has spun off its generative artificial intelligence team into a company of its own. It is called **Motherbrain**, and the announcement came on 19 November at 17:45 Italian time, in the press release on the results for the first half of the 2026-27 financial year.

## What Motherbrain, Ubisoft's AI subsidiary, will do

Motherbrain is the group that created Teammates, which Ubisoft describes as its first playable experience based on generative AI. It now becomes a subsidiary dedicated to developing proprietary technology for new forms of gameplay.

The press release names three areas of work for now: new player experiences, "smart" NPCs with memory, and AI-run game masters. The subsidiary will work for Ubisoft's creatives but also for external studios, and the publisher plans partnerships with a few selected technology companies.

**Yves Guillemot**, co-founder and CEO, presented Motherbrain as a way to give creatives new tools and offer players richer experiences. The location, head and headcount of the new company have not been disclosed.

## Ubisoft's half-year figures

In the first half, net bookings (the main sales indicator used by Ubisoft) came to €644.7 million, down 16.5% on the previous year. The second quarter reached €388.9 million, above the forecast of around €370 million.

The quarter was driven mainly by Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced, which according to Ubisoft exceeded expectations during its launch period. For the full financial year the publisher confirms net bookings down by a high single-digit percentage and expects around €250 million in the third quarter.
