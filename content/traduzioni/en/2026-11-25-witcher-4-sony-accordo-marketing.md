**The Witcher 4** will have PlayStation at its side in the promotional campaign. On 24 November, while presenting its results for the third quarter of 2026, **CD Projekt** announced that it has signed new cooperation agreements with **Sony Interactive Entertainment**, which also cover the marketing of the new chapter in the saga.

## What the agreement between CD Projekt and PlayStation covers

The agreements, signed on 24 November itself, cover three areas: the presence of CD Projekt games on PlayStation platforms, their inclusion in the PlayStation Plus subscription catalogues and cooperation on the campaign for The Witcher 4. This is according to the company's financial report, cited by the Polish site Gram.pl.

During the investor presentation, chief financial officer Piotr Nielubowicz mentioned, as reported by VGC, "our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign", adding that the deal will support the company's results this year and in the years ahead.

## The Witcher 4 on PlayStation: a marketing exclusive like GTA 6?

Many people were quick to draw the comparison with GTA 6, which had Sony as its promotional partner. CD Projekt, however, did not use the word exclusive and did not explain what the campaign will involve. So it is not known whether there will be dedicated content, bundles or trailers shown at PlayStation events. For now these are only guesses.

## When The Witcher 4 comes out

In its quarterly results statement, CD Projekt confirms The Witcher 4's release in 2028, without a more precise date. In the third quarter the group posted 208 million złoty in revenue and 109 million in net profit.

The Polish studio had already made headlines about the saga a few days ago: for [Witcher 3 Songs of the Past it used AI to touch up Geralt's Polish voice](/articoli/witcher-3-songs-of-the-past-ia-geralt/), with the actor's consent.
