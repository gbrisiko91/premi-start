After nine years of waiting, **Wolfenstein 3** is finally official. On 12 November, during the closing evening of the celebrations for **Xbox**'s 25th anniversary in Seattle, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma confirmed that the new instalment is in production at **MachineGames**, the Swedish studio that brought the series back to life with The New Order. Brian Bloom returns as BJ Blazkowicz and Debra Wilson as Grace Walker.

There is no trailer and no date: for now we have the confirmation and a couple of behind-the-scenes photos.

## Wolfenstein 3 is in production: what Xbox announced

Sharma made the announcement with a post on X, immediately picked up by the MachineGames account with a themed message: BJ's reputation, the studio writes, precedes him. The official recap published on [XBOX Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/) confirms that the game is in production and that Bloom and Wilson are reprising their roles.

Two images were shared: a page from a script and a shot from a motion capture session in which, according to several outlets, the two actors themselves can be recognised. Some fans have read the name of the first chapter on the script, but that is their interpretation and there is nothing official.

## When Wolfenstein 3 comes out and on which platforms

Xbox has not committed on this: there is no release date, launch window or platforms. Looking at Bethesda's most recent games, it is reasonable to expect a release on PC and Xbox Series X|S, perhaps with a PS5 version, but for now it is only a guess.

In February, an alleged casting call leaked online mentioned a new character, a Ukrainian orphan, and a release no earlier than 2027. The official announcement confirms neither detail, so they should be treated as rumours.

## Why BJ Blazkowicz's return is making so much noise

The last numbered instalment, Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus, came out in 2017 and ended with an open-ended story, in a 1960s America dominated by the Nazi regime. Since then MachineGames has released the co-op spin-off Youngblood (2019), starring BJ's twin daughters, and then devoted itself to Indiana Jones and the Great Circle. The studio head had said several times that he wanted to complete the trilogy, and now the third instalment is confirmed.

Not everyone liked the way the announcement arrived. Part of the international press pointed out that a social media post and a photo of the script are not much compared with a trailer on stage, and say nothing about how far along development is.

On the same day Sharma also confirmed that id Software is working on a new project, while DOOM: The Dark Ages will get Slaughter mode on 16 November. Brian Bloom, the voice and face of BJ in the previous instalments, returns, together with Debra Wilson in the role of Grace Walker.
