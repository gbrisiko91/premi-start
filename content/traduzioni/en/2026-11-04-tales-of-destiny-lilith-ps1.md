**Tales of Destiny** had been keeping a secret for almost thirty years. A group of fans has discovered how to recruit **Lilith Aileros**, the sister of protagonist **Stahn**, in the original **PlayStation** version of **Namco**'s JRPG, where until now it was taken for granted that it was impossible. The method, shown in a YouTube video, requires 26 steps and almost obsessive care from the very first hours of play.

## Who discovered how to recruit Lilith in Tales of Destiny

The discovery is credited to YouTuber **Kevangelion**, who published footage of the recruitment scene on the American version of the game, thanking users SymphoniaLauren, Mziab and Ethanol for their help. The starting point was an old post on a Japanese forum describing the procedure: Kevangelion put it to the test, verified it and turned it into a step-by-step guide.

The reason Lilith had never been seen in the party is precisely that the procedure is extremely long and full of pitfalls. Many of the conditions must be met hours before the moment the character can join the team, in the second half of the adventure, and a single mistake is enough to lose the chance forever.

## The conditions for getting Lilith in your party on PS1

The full guide has 26 steps. According to the sources, the most important rules are these:

- **don't take items from villagers' houses** (dungeon loot, on the other hand, is fine);
- don't pick up food or **Apple Gel** in the early areas;
- **don't manually change Stahn's weapon** in the first part of the game;
- don't sell or throw away the **Silver Ladle**, the silver ladle that is Lilith's weapon;
- don't interrupt or skip certain conversations and don't anger characters, for example by getting told off by **Rutee** or annoying certain merchants;
- **talk to Lilith only once** in the village of Leene.

In practice, you have to play as an impeccable hero: no stealing from wardrobes, no excess chatter. Quite an irony for a genre in which rummaging through every house is almost a habit.

## Why the Tales of Destiny secret stayed hidden for so long

Tales of Destiny came out in Japan in December **1997** and arrived in North America in 1998: it's the second instalment in the **Tales of** series, now owned by **Bandai Namco**, and it was never officially released in Europe on PS1. In the later **PlayStation 2** re-releases, which came out only in Japan, Lilith was given a bigger role, but in the original version nobody had ever managed to bring her into the team in a documented way.

The case is a reminder of how '90s games can still surprise us: between unexplored code and hidden conditions, hunting for secrets is one of the souls of retro gaming. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you recruit Lilith in Tales of Destiny on PS1?

Yes: by meeting a long series of conditions, Lilith can join the party in the second half of the game. The method has only now been documented, 29 years after release.

### Who is Lilith Aileros in Tales of Destiny?

She is the younger sister of Stahn, the protagonist. She fights with a ladle and in the PS1 version was considered a non-playable character.

### Is Tales of Destiny available on modern consoles?

At the moment the PS1 version hasn't been re-released on current consoles: to play it you need the original hardware or a period copy.
