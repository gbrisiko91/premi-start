**Fortnite**'s **Pirate Shop Token** has become the most sought-after item of the moment. Introduced with the **Treasure Hunters** mini-season, the one featuring the **One Piece** crossover, it unlocks almost the entire Item Shop for free for 14 days. It's so rare that accounts have appeared for sale on eBay for up to $10,000, and **Epic Games** has had to step in.

## What is Fortnite's Pirate Shop Token

The token is found in the treasure chests, the ones marked with a skull, scattered across the Battle Royale map. Whoever finds it gets access to the Pirate Shop: for two weeks they can take skins, emotes and all the items normally bought with **V-Bucks** for free, and keep them forever.

There are a few exclusions: no gifts to friends, no Battle Pass levels and no items that can only be bought with real money. According to Kotaku and Push Square, access is split into two 14-day periods, one to use in 2026 and one in 2027. One detail has fired up squads: when a player finds the token, the reward also goes to their teammates.

## How rare the token is and why it's worth so much

Epic hasn't published the official drop rate. The community estimates that one token drops roughly every **700,000 chests**, an unconfirmed figure but enough to turn the mini-season into a lottery. Nobody knows for sure how many players have already found it.

Hence the rush of the last few days. Some players are blowing open locked chests in vaults with grenades and cannonballs, some are organising into groups to multiply their chances, and some are offering paid "carries". Forums and social media are also circulating automatic farming programs and overlays that reveal chests, all banned by the game's rules.

## The account black market and Epic Games' response

Since the token stays tied to the account that found it, those looking to cash in sell the whole profile. Listings between $1,000 and $10,000 have appeared on eBay, as reported by Kotaku, Multiplayer.it and Everyeye.

The official Fortnite account on X responded on 3 November: buying accounts with the token or using tools to find it is a scam. The token drops from chests like any other loot, so according to Epic every player has the same chance. Remember that buying and selling accounts violates the terms of service and can lead to a ban, on top of the very real risk of paying and receiving nothing.

The mini-season runs until 4 December, so the hunt has only just begun. The issue of content rules in games is a hot topic elsewhere too, as we saw with [itch.io removing AI-generated games](/articoli/itch-io-giochi-ia-eliminati/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you find the Pirate Shop Token in Fortnite?

It's found by opening the treasure chests marked with the skull symbol in Battle Royale. There's no guaranteed method: it's a random and very rare drop.

### How long does the free Pirate Shop last?

Access lasts 14 days from activation. Items claimed during that period stay in your locker forever.

### Is it worth buying an account with the token?

No. Epic calls it a scam and selling accounts violates the terms of service, with the risk of losing both your money and your account.
