**Analogue** released **3DOS 1.6.0** firmware for the **Analogue 3D** on 18 November. The FPGA console plays original Nintendo 64 cartridges, and the headline addition is Wi-Fi: the console can now update itself without going through a computer.

## Analogue 3D firmware 1.6.0: what's new

Until now, updating the Analogue 3D meant downloading the firmware on a PC and copying it to an SD card. Version 1.6.0 adds dual-band Wi-Fi support (5 GHz, 2.4 GHz and mixed networks, including hidden ones), and 3DOS now keeps itself up to date automatically.

Setup starts right after installation, or from the Settings menu under Wi-Fi. Once connected, the console can also set the date and time from the internet (Settings, System, Date and Time).

## Optional statistics and fixes

The connection also brings an option to send anonymous usage data to Analogue. It stays off until you accept it and can be managed from Settings, About, Privacy.

The release notes also list more regions to choose from for the time zone, a few visual tweaks to the interface and two fixes: a bug that sometimes stopped the game from being displayed until the in-game menu was opened, and the USA region tab not appearing in the library. The package weighs 21.9 MB and is available from Analogue's support page.
