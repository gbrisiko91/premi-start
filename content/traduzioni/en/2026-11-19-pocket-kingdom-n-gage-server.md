**Pocket Kingdom: Own the World**, the online role-playing game released in 2004 for the **Nokia N-Gage**, can be played online again. It was announced on 16 November by developer **Michael Fitzmayer**, who together with his team added full support for the game to PhantomLink and launched a permanent server.

## Pocket Kingdom for N-Gage, the pocket-sized heir to Dragon Force

Developed by io-Spiral, Pocket Kingdom is one of the Sega games released on the N-Gage. It arrived in North America on 24 November 2004, and in Europe five days later. It was an MMO designed for a gaming phone, with an offline mode and an online part that ran through the N-Gage Arena service: there you could find other players and attack one another.

The game is considered a spiritual successor to Dragon Force, the 1996 strategy role-playing game for the Sega Saturn. Reviews at the time were decent, but the N-Gage's limited reach did not help it find an audience, and its online services had been switched off for years.

## How to play Pocket Kingdom online again

Fitzmayer invites anyone who wants to try it to join the Discord server dedicated to the Nokia N-Gage, where the community discusses emulation and preservation of the console. The post does not list any technical requirements, so instructions should be requested there. Fitzmayer also maintains a development kit for the N-Gage, documented on his website mupf.dev.

This is not the first recent case of a community bringing an abandoned game's multiplayer back to life: earlier this month it happened with the [mod that restores Mass Effect 3's multiplayer](/articoli/mass-effect-3-multiplayer-mod/) in the Legendary Edition.
