This time we're not summarising press releases: we've **played GTA 6** ourselves on our PS5 and recorded the experience. We can't say exactly how many hours we've spent with it, but we're almost at the end of the first chapter. That's enough for some initial impressions, not a final verdict. That's why the score below is provisional.

## The first thing you notice: the graphics

The moment you take control, GTA 6 looks remarkably real. The image quality is impressive; at times you almost forget you're holding a controller. Rockstar already raised the bar with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, but the leap forward here is obvious.

We played in Performance mode. Even so, we noticed drops in smoothness while driving.

## Driving and shooting: cumbersome, but better than GTA V

Both driving and gunplay feel cumbersome at first, and take some getting used to. Compared with GTA V, however, they feel much better: cars behave more believably and shootouts feel more considered. That weight seems deliberate, much like in Red Dead Redemption 2.

Our videos include missions, free exploration, driving and combat. We also unlocked our first trophy, “Come in paradiso”, a story-related bronze awarded during one of the early missions in chapter one.

## Leonida: lots of traffic, few pedestrians

Leonida's roads are full of cars, sometimes excessively so, and the American-metropolis atmosphere works. Yet very few people seem to be walking around. The often-empty pavements clash with a city that should feel chaotic. We'll see whether this changes in other areas as we progress.

## Jason and Lucia: the jury is still out

We're reserving judgement on the two protagonists. At first glance, Jason and Lucia seem like a slightly artificial couple who haven't quite convinced us. We're still early in the story, though, and Rockstar's narratives often take time to find their rhythm.

## The biggest drawback: a map that's too large

What we liked least is one of Rockstar's biggest selling points: size. The map is huge, and after a while travelling from one place to another becomes tiresome. The journey doesn't always feel rewarding. In chapter one, at least so far, all that space mainly means more roads to cross.

## Our provisional score

GTA 6 has impressed us. It's gorgeous and feels better to play than GTA V. But the dips in smoothness while driving, the empty pavements and the exhausting size of the map keep us from calling it a masterpiece just yet.

**Soffia Cartuccia provisional score: 8.5/10**
