**Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea** is an arcade naval battle game with one clear strength: it plays **offline**, with no need for a connection. At the helm is **Giancarlo Mennillo**, a young man from the province of **Salerno** who created it on his own, and we've seen it in action.

## How to play Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea

The view is top-down, like the arcade shooters of old. You steer a pirate ship with a virtual joystick and fight using two action buttons, while enemy ships appear at sea, each with its health points clearly visible above its sails.

At the top of the screen there are three numbers to keep an eye on: **gold coins**, remaining **cannonballs** and the **damage** bar. Ammunition runs out, so you can't fire aimlessly: the chests floating among the waves replenish gold and shots. The rocks scattered across the map, on the other hand, are to be avoided.

## The weather in Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea changes the gameplay

The most successful part is the changing sea. The early stages of a match are played under clear skies, but as you progress, past roughly 9,000 points, the weather starts to change. And it's not just a matter of atmosphere: each condition changes the way you play.

- **Snow** slows the ship down.
- **Fog** reduces visibility: enemies and obstacles become faded silhouettes and you only see them coming at the last moment.
- **Wind** pushes chests, bombs and even your own ship in the direction it's blowing.
- **Storms** are the most dangerous: during thunderstorms lightning can strike your ship and deal 40 points of damage.

And then there's him: a **megalodon** that crosses the scene and attacks whoever happens to be in range.

## Ships to collect and daily rewards

Outside of battle, the **Le Mie Navi** (My Ships) section gathers the ships you've unlocked. Each has four stats: speed, firepower, ammunition and health points. Some ships only appear for a limited time, like Halloween's **Pumpkin Phantom**, with its orange pumpkin-shaped sails.

From the main menu you can access the **shop**, the **Top Pirati** (Top Pirates) leaderboard and the feature for inviting friends, and there's also a link to **Discord**. Every day, when you open the game, you receive a reward of 500 gold coins.

## Why offline play matters

Many smartphone games today demand a constant connection, even when you're playing alone. Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea goes in the opposite direction: you can play it on the train, on a plane or wherever there's no signal, with no waiting and no dependence on a server.

It's also a great Italian story. There's no studio behind it, just a young man who built his game from scratch. And the question of who makes games, and how, is more relevant than ever, as shown by [itch.io's clean-up of mass-produced AI games](/articoli/itch-io-giochi-ia-eliminati/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you play Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea without the internet?

Yes, that's its strong point: matches are played offline, with no need for a connection.

### Who created Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea?

It was created by Giancarlo Mennillo, an independent developer from the province of Salerno.

### How does the weather work in Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea?

After the early stages of a match the weather changes, and each condition has an effect: snow slows the ship, fog reduces visibility, wind moves the ship and objects, and in storms lightning deals 40 points of damage.

### What can you play Pirates: Black Sails of the Sea on?

It's a smartphone game with touch controls: a virtual joystick and on-screen buttons.
