**The Duskbloods** has been delayed to **2027**. **FromSoftware** announced it on 29 October: the **Nintendo Switch 2** exclusive, broadly expected in 2026, will need a few more months. There is no release day or month yet.

## Why The Duskbloods has been delayed

The announcement came in a message on the game's official X account. The studio explains that the project is in its final polishing phase and that, after gathering player feedback during the **August network test**, it wants to spend more time on the game rules and balancing.

The stated goals are three: better combat, more strategic depth and more freedom for players to express their own style.

## When The Duskbloods comes out on Switch 2

The official window is now 2027, as also confirmed by the [game's page on the Nintendo eShop](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/the-duskbloods-switch-2/). FromSoftware and Nintendo have not given a month: the exact date will probably arrive in the coming months, perhaps during a Nintendo Direct.

## What The Duskbloods is

Announced in April 2025 during the Nintendo Direct dedicated to Switch 2, The Duskbloods is a **PvPvE** action game with online play at its core: matches support up to **eight players**, who take on the role of the "Bloodsworn", people who have become stronger than humans thanks to special blood. It is developed by FromSoftware, the studio led by **Hidetaka Miyazaki** and famous for Dark Souls, Bloodborne and Elden Ring. As it is the studio's first project built around multiplayer from the ground up, it is easy to see why the August online test carried so much weight.

## Why the delay is causing debate

The Duskbloods is FromSoftware's first game designed as an exclusive for a Nintendo console and, ever since the first trailer, many fans have compared it to a spiritual successor to **Bloodborne**. In recent weeks fears of a delay had grown, not least because until a few months ago the studio and its parent company Kadokawa had reaffirmed a 2026 release.

The delay robs Switch 2 of one of the most anticipated titles of its second holiday season. There is a positive sign too, though: the explicit reference to the August test suggests that community feedback has genuinely shaped the studio's work. On the console front, the case of [Modern Warfare 4 and the Itaewon footage](/articoli/modern-warfare-4-filmati-itaewon/) is also still a hot topic.
