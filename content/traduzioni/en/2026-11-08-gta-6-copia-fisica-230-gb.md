Eleven days before release, the first **physical copy of GTA 6** has appeared online. The photos, posted on Reddit on 7 November by a German user who reportedly works for a retail chain, show the **PS5** box of **Grand Theft Auto VI**. The detail that has sparked debate is on the back: the required space is **230 GB**, 18 more than the 212 stated so far by **Rockstar Games**.

These are unofficial images, but the material has been analysed by several outlets, including Multiplayer.it, Everyeye.it and the fan site Rockstar Intel, and it matches what Rockstar has already said about the physical editions.

## What's inside the GTA 6 physical box

The box is a regular plastic case with an inner cover, not a cardboard voucher. Inside, however, there is no disc: as already announced, the physical editions contain a **download code**. It's a decision that caused a lot of debate among collectors in June and is now becoming reality.

Other details emerge from the photos:

- the box indicates a **single-player** game;
- the back shows an online features icon whose meaning is unclear: it could refer to a new GTA Online or to buying the upgrade to the Ultimate Edition;
- the box features three never-before-seen images, with **Jason**, **Lucia** and a plane flying over the islands.

German chain MediaMarkt has also shown a **double-sided poster**, which according to the retailer will be included in all code copies. The map of the game world, on the other hand, reportedly won't be in the box.

## Is GTA 6 212 or 230 GB?

The official figure given by Rockstar for the preload is around 212 GB. The 230 GB printed on the box could include the extra space needed to unpack the files during installation, or take into account a day-one patch. Neither hypothesis has been confirmed.

The practical advice doesn't change: it's best to free up at least 230 GB on your SSD. On a 512 GB **Xbox Series S**, that means devoting almost two thirds of the available space to GTA 6. All the details on times and pre-downloading are in our article on [the GTA 6 preload and how much space to free up](/articoli/gta-6-preload-peso/).

## When GTA 6 physical copies arrive in shops

The photos confirm that the boxes are already reaching retailers. Sales of the physical code editions are scheduled from **12 November**: from then on it will be possible to redeem the code and start downloading. Those who bought digitally can instead preload from **10 November**, as announced on the [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10).

Release remains set for **19 November 2026** on PS5 and **Xbox Series X|S**. There is no date yet for the PC version.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the physical version of GTA 6 have a disc?

No. The physical editions of GTA 6 contain a code to download the game, with no disc. So you still need an internet connection and space on your SSD.

### How much space does GTA 6 need?

Rockstar states around 212 GB, but the physical box says 230 GB. To be safe, it's worth freeing up at least 230.

### When do GTA 6 physical copies come out?

The code editions arrive in shops from 12 November 2026; the game unlocks on 19 November.
