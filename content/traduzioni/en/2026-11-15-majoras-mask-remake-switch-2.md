Kit Ellis and Krysta Yang, former marketing and public relations leads at **Nintendo of America**, think a remake of **The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask** for **Nintendo Switch 2** is plausible following the success of *Ocarina of Time*. It is their prediction, not a Nintendo announcement or confirmed inside information. Complicating the picture is the opposite claim from Shpeshal Nick, who says the project is not currently in production.

## Why two ex-Nintendo staff think a Majora's Mask remake is likely

According to GamesRadar+'s write-up of the *Kit & Krysta* podcast episode, Yang believes Nintendo may already have started work, because the *Ocarina of Time* remake created a technical foundation that could potentially be reused. Ellis is more cautious: even if a new *Majora's Mask* did arrive, it would not necessarily be the next entry in the series and could still take time. These are their personal assessments; they did not present any documents or announcements proving the project exists.

The theory also stems from the history of the two original games. *Majora's Mask* came out on Nintendo 64 in 2000, less than two years after *Ocarina of Time*. In the official *Iwata Asks* interview series, Eiji Aonuma explains that development was able to reuse models and assets made for its predecessor. That precedent explains Ellis and Yang's reasoning, but it does not prove that Nintendo is repeating the same strategy on Switch 2.

## Shpeshal Nick's opposing claim, still unconfirmed

On 10 November, insider **Shpeshal Nick** wrote on X that, according to one of his sources, a *Majora's Mask* remake is not in any stage of production. He claimed the source is the same one that gave him details about the *Ocarina of Time* remake. This is the insider's claim, **not an official denial from Nintendo**, and it cannot be independently verified through the public information available.

The discussion had already been fuelled by possible references to masks in the new *Ocarina of Time* and by theories from creator Austin John Plays. Nick also claims that a planned third amiibo would depict Ganondorf, not a *Majora's Mask* character. This detail also remains a rumour: none of the elements mentioned confirms either the development or the cancellation of a remake.

## What is confirmed: sales, history and available versions

The *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake for Switch 2 launched on **5 November 2026**. According to [Nintendo's official press release](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/release/en/2026/261111.html) of 11 November, it sold **more than 5.2 million copies worldwide in its first four days**, counting physical and digital copies. It is a real figure, but it does not amount to confirmation of any follow-up.

As for *Majora's Mask*, Nintendo confirms in the *Iwata Asks* interviews that the original came out in **Japan in April 2000** and in **Europe in November 2000**. Today the Nintendo 64 version is included in the **Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack** library, where it has been available since **25 February 2022**; there is also *Majora's Mask 3D*, a remake released for Nintendo 3DS in 2015.
