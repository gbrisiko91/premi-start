**Control Resonant** is the fastest-selling game in **Remedy Entertainment**'s history. The Finnish studio behind **Alan Wake** and **Max Payne** confirmed it on 3 November in its third-quarter 2026 report: the sequel to Control passed **one million copies** in three weeks and pushed revenue for the period up by **226%**.

## Control Resonant sales in numbers

Control Resonant launched digitally on **24 September** on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and physically on 15 October. It was on 15 October that it crossed the one-million-copy mark. According to CEO **Jean-Charles Gaudechon**, who took the helm of the studio this year, around **half of the players** are new to the series: an important figure for a brand that, by its own admission, had so far sold less than it deserved.

It is also the first game of this scale that Remedy has published on its own, without an external publisher, on an IP entirely its own. Gaudechon called it a decisive milestone for the company.

## Remedy's third-quarter 2026 results

The figures for the July–September quarter clearly show the Resonant effect:

- **revenue**: €39.6 million, compared with €12.2 million in the same period of 2025;
- **game sales and royalties**: €35.3 million, up from €6 million a year ago;
- **operating profit**: €0.6 million, after a loss of €16.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, weighed down by the write-down of **FBC: Firebreak**;
- **EBITDA**: €6.8 million, compared with €0.7 million.

In nine months Remedy has already brought in €62.9 million, more than the €59.5 million for the whole of 2025, although the nine-month operating result remains slightly in the red. The studio confirms that it expects 2026 revenue and EBITDA to grow compared with last year.

## Why Control Resonant's success matters

The result comes in an extremely crowded autumn, just weeks before **GTA 6**, and at a time when many studios are closing or cutting staff, as the recent [closure of BioWare](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/) shows. After the flop of FBC: Firebreak, Remedy needed a hit to prove it can stand on its own two feet as a publisher.

## What to expect from Remedy

The next big project is the **Max Payne 1 and 2 remake**, published by Rockstar Games and now in full production. There is no date yet, but healthy finances give the studio more room to work on its next games.
