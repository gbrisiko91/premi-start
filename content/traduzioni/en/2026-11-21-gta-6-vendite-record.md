**GTA 6** took $3.79 billion on its first day. **Take-Two Interactive** and **Rockstar Games** announced the figure on 20 November in a press release issued from New York: it covers worldwide retail sales up to the end of 19 November, release day, and is a company estimate.

## GTA 6 sales: the numbers in Take-Two's press release

Before it had even reached the shops, GTA 6 had already sold 32.5 million units across all platforms, and Take-Two says this too is a new record.

On the $3.79 billion figure, the publisher looks beyond video games and speaks of a new record "for entertainment in any form". This is Rockstar's claim, based on internal numbers that nobody has yet verified independently.

Some outlets wrote "revenue" or "net sales", but the press release refers to first-day retail sales, meaning how much players spent.

## GTA 6 and the numbers of the Grand Theft Auto series

In the same press release Take-Two looks back at the history of the series. GTA 5 has shipped almost 235 million copies, the whole Grand Theft Auto franchise stands at almost 480 million and the Red Dead Redemption series is approaching 118 million.
