**Wave Race: Blue Storm** joins the GameCube catalogue of **Nintendo Switch Online**. **Nintendo** has announced that the jet ski racing game, released on **GameCube** in 2001, is playable from 24 November on Switch 2 for subscribers with the Expansion Pack.

## How to play Wave Race: Blue Storm on Switch 2

The game is in the Nintendo Classics GameCube app, which is exclusive to Switch 2 and to Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack members. You need to update the app for it to appear. Anyone with only the basic subscription or a first-generation Switch is left out, as with the other GameCube titles.

## Weather that changes the race

Blue Storm is the sequel to Wave Race 64 and can be played against opponents or against your own times. Its standout feature is the weather: in the official presentation Nintendo notes that the sky can shift from sunshine to storms, and that conditions change visibility, water level and wave height during the race.

Waves swelling mid-race were its trademark twenty years ago, and on a modern TV they still look the part.

## The other GameCube games on Switch 2

Wave Race: Blue Storm joins a library that already includes, among others, The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker, F-Zero GX, Super Mario Sunshine, Luigi's Mansion, Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance, Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness and SoulCalibur II.
