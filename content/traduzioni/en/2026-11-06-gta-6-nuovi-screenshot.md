**Rockstar Games** has released a new collection of **GTA 6 screenshots**: it is called the **Launch Gallery** and has been live since 5 November on the official **Grand Theft Auto VI** website. There are nearly 40 images (outlets' counts range from 35 to 39), showing **Jason**, **Lucia**, several supporting characters and many corners of the state of **Leonida**. The game launches in two weeks, on 19 November, on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**.

## Where to see the new GTA 6 screenshots

The gallery is on the [official Grand Theft Auto VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery), announced in a Rockstar Newswire post. Rockstar has not given an exact number of images nor added captions: that is why VGC talks about "nearly 40" screenshots, while TweakTown and PC Gamer count 39.

The images are designed to be viewed full screen and, according to several outlets, are also available in the media section of Rockstar's website.

## What the images show: Jason, Lucia and Leonida

The new images are mostly **cinematic**: carefully composed shots that look more like a poster than a gameplay screen. The two protagonists are still front and centre, pictured amid robberies, high-speed chases, police raids, nightclubs and backwater bars.

There are quieter moments too, showing off the variety of the map: underwater dives, sunsets over grassland, and the inevitable alligator for a game set in a version of Florida. Among the shots that have sparked the most discussion on social media is one of Jason in tactical gear stepping off a military plane, an image that hints at missions very different from the classic city heists.

Anyone hoping for new gameplay details will be left empty-handed. With no captions, mission names and story context remain a mystery until launch. To see the game in motion, you still have to go back to the gameplay presentation broadcast in August.

## When GTA 6 comes out and what happens between now and launch

The schedule is now packed. The **preload** for digital copies starts on **10 November**, while physical editions with a code arrive in shops from 12 November: we explained the times and the space required in our article on the [GTA 6 preload and the 212 GB you need to free up](/articoli/gta-6-preload-peso/). The release is set for **19 November 2026**, on consoles only.

There is still no date for the PC version: VGC expects it to come some time after the console release. In the meantime, according to VGC, Rockstar should release a trailer on launch day, in keeping with the tradition of its big games.

The Launch Gallery strategy is typical Rockstar: show a lot, explain little. It is an approach that has already worked with previous waves of images, analysed frame by frame by fans hunting for details about the map and characters.
