**Shigeru Miyamoto** will be the first recipient of the Legend Award, a new honour from the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences (AIAS). The announcement came on 16 November: the award will be presented to him during the thirtieth **D.I.C.E. Awards**, on Thursday 18 February 2027 at the Cosmopolitan in Las Vegas.

## What the D.I.C.E. Legend Award is

According to the AIAS, the award celebrates an industry icon whose vision continues to redefine interactive entertainment, a figure who has already achieved universal and lasting recognition. The most curious rule is its frequency: the Legend Award will be given at most once every ten years. AIAS president Meggan Scavio said that Miyamoto "perfectly embodies" the spirit of the new award.

The Legend Award joins the Academy's other special honours, such as the Hall of Fame, the Lifetime Achievement and the Pioneer Award.

## Miyamoto and the D.I.C.E., a story that starts in 1998

For the AIAS it is a return. In 1998 Miyamoto was the first name inducted into the Academy's Hall of Fame, as shown by the special awards archive on the official website.

Having joined **Nintendo** in 1977 after graduating from Kanazawa College of Art, Miyamoto is the creator of Super Mario and The Legend of Zelda and is now Representative Director and Fellow of the company. In October the Japanese government named him among the recipients of the Order of Culture, the country's highest cultural honour: it is the first time for a figure from the video game industry.

In Italy the 18 February ceremony will fall during the night: 8 pm in Las Vegas corresponds to 5 am on 19 February. Anyone who wants to revisit his 1998 masterpiece can read [the reviews of the Ocarina of Time remake](/articoli/zelda-ocarina-of-time-recensioni/).
