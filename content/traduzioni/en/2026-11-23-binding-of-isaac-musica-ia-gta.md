The GTA-themed run added to **The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+** had a soundtrack generated with artificial intelligence, and nobody had disclosed it. Players found out a few days after the update, Steam reviews collapsed and **Nicalis** pulled the tracks with a patch released within hours.

## The Binding of Isaac's GTA mode

The update is the one that brought Repentance+ to PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2 on 19 November. Entering the seed "V1CE BSMT" unlocks a daily run inspired by Vice City, with its own songs. A fun idea, released right in the days of the GTA 6 launch.

## How the AI music came to light

A Reddit user, ShweetsStuff, extracted the game's audio files and found the words "made with suno" in the metadata, referring to the music generation service Suno. According to Dexerto, there are seven songs plus a few intros and outros.

The Steam page for Repentance+ carries no disclosure about the use of AI. The backlash came in the form of negative reviews: when we checked it on the morning of 23 November, the page showed only 8% positive among the 1,205 reviews from the last 30 days, with an "Overwhelmingly Negative" rating.

## What Edmund McMillen and Nicalis said

**Edmund McMillen**, the creator of Isaac, wrote on X on 21 November that he had woken up to chaos and did not know the daily run used generated music. "Obviously I had no idea", he added, promising to find out what had happened and to have the tracks removed.

A few hours later Nicalis published patch notes that include removing the generated music files. On PC, according to the studio, the fix should already be live, while for consoles it has been submitted to Microsoft, Nintendo and Sony with no release date. The studio did not explain how those tracks ended up in the game.

Other stores are also taking action on games made with AI: earlier this month [itch.io deleted around two terabytes of generated games](/articoli/itch-io-giochi-ia-eliminati/) and extended its anti-AI filter.
