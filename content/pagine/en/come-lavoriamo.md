## How we pick stories

Every morning we check the main Italian and international video game outlets, the official channels of publishers and platforms (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam and game studios) and the most-followed discussions on Reddit and YouTube. We give each story a **Hype** score from 0 to 100 and pick the ones people are talking about most, mixing consoles, PC, industry and retro gaming.

We don't write about deals and discounts, we don't publish sponsored content and we skip rumours that have no credible source.

## How we fact-check

- Every important fact must be confirmed by **at least two independent sources** or by an **official source**.
- **Rumours** and leaks are always flagged as such, in the headline or the first paragraph.
- At the end of every article you'll find the list of **sources**, with links, so you can check for yourself.
- We write in our own words: from other outlets we take at most a short quote, always in quotation marks and credited.

## Artificial intelligence: how we use it

Let's be clear: to find the day's news and prepare a first draft we use **artificial intelligence** tools. Every draft is read, checked and edited by a person before it's published, and no article goes live automatically.

The **other language versions** of the site (English, German, Spanish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, Japanese and Russian) are translated automatically with the help of AI from the Italian original. If anything is unclear or differs, the Italian version prevails; it is always linked at the end of every translated article.

## Images

We only use **official** images: press materials from publishers and platforms, official game pages on Steam, Nintendo eShop, PlayStation Store or Xbox Wire, or freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons. Under every image we say who it belongs to.

We don't use photos taken from other outlets, photos of real people without permission, or images linked to tragedies or crime news.

## Mistakes and corrections

Mistakes happen. When they do, we fix the article as soon as possible and, if the error changes the meaning of the story, we note it at the end of the text with the date of the update. If something doesn't add up, write to us from the [Contact]({{LINK:contatti}}) page: we read every report.
