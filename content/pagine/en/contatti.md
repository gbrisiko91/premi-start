## Email

The easiest way to reach us is to write to **[{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}})**. We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days. You can write in English: our main language is Italian, but we'll do our best.

## What you can write to us about

- **Tips**: seen a story we haven't covered? Send us the link to the source.
- **Corrections**: if an article contains a mistake, tell us what it is and, if you can, a source that proves it. We fix it as soon as possible, as explained in [How we work]({{LINK:come-lavoriamo}}).
- **Developers and publishers**: press releases, press kits and review codes are welcome, especially from Italian and independent studios. Receiving a press release doesn't mean we'll cover it, and we don't accept paid articles.
- **Collaborations**: if you'd like to write for us or propose a project, tell us who you are and what you have in mind.

## Privacy and data

For questions about your personal data, see our [privacy policy]({{LINK:privacy}}) or write to the address above.
