The King is back in sight, at least on Steam. The **Duke Nukem Forever** page has been quietly updated with footage and images that, according to fans, come from the **unreleased 2000 and 2006 builds**, versions of the game that stayed locked away during its fourteen years of development. That was enough to start a rumour: **2K** is supposedly preparing an official release of an old version. For now, though, it is only a theory, with no confirmation whatsoever.

## What has changed on Duke Nukem Forever's Steam page

It was fans who noticed first, flagging a new trailer and GIFs taken from the 2006 build on social media. The story was picked up by **Time Extension**, which speaks of material from unreleased versions of the game.

Steam's public data also confirm a recent change: according to **SteamDB**, the graphical assets on the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/57900/) were modified on 10 November. The publisher and developer remain 2K and **Gearbox Software**, both now under the **Take-Two** umbrella.

## Why the old builds have fans dreaming

Duke Nukem Forever is the legend of endless development. Announced by **3D Realms** in 1997 as the follow-up to **Duke Nukem 3D**, it went through different graphics engines, was restarted from scratch several times and, after the studio's collapse in 2009, was finished by Gearbox. It only came out in 2011, to very lukewarm reviews.

For many fans, the real Duke Nukem Forever is the one they never played: the versions shown in trailers in the early 2000s, which had little to do with the final game. In 2022 a 2001 build leaked online and became a minor sensation among fans of '90s shooters, complete with fan projects to make it playable. An official release, perhaps 30 years after Duke Nukem 3D's debut, would be a big event for retro gaming. ## What to expect: a rumour, not an announcement

To be clear: neither 2K nor Gearbox has announced anything. The page update could simply be a refresh of the promotional material. Time Extension itself describes the idea of a re-release of the old build as pure fan speculation. We will keep following the story and update this article if confirmation arrives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Duke Nukem Forever on Steam?

Yes, Duke Nukem Forever has been available on Steam for PC since 2011, in the version completed by Gearbox.

### What is the 2001 build of Duke Nukem Forever?

It is a development version of the game made by 3D Realms, very different from the one released in 2011; it leaked online in 2022 and has never been officially released.

### Will 2K release the old versions of Duke Nukem Forever?

There is no confirmation: for now it is only a fan theory, sparked by the Steam page update.
