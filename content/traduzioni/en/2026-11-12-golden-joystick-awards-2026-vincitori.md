The **Golden Joystick Awards 2026** have their Game of the Year: it is **Resident Evil Requiem** from **Capcom**, crowned Ultimate Game of the Year in London on 11 November. It was a triumphant night for the Osaka-based company, which also took home Studio of the Year, while the real surprise was the small **Meccha Chameleon**, with four awards.

## Golden Joystick Awards 2026: Resident Evil Requiem sweeps the board

The Golden Joysticks are among the industry's longest-running awards, organised by **GamesRadar+** and largely voted for by the public. This year Requiem won in every major category it was nominated in:

- **Ultimate Game of the Year**;
- **Console Game of the Year**;
- **Best Game Trailer**, for the "Evil Has Always Had a Name" trailer;
- **Best Lead Performer**, for Angela Sant'Albano, the voice and face of Grace Ashcroft.

On top of these comes **Studio of the Year** for Capcom, which in 2026 also lined up **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** (best audio) and **Pragmata**, honoured for Grace Saif's performance as best supporting actress.

## Meccha Chameleon, the surprise of the Golden Joysticks

The most-awarded game alongside Requiem is **Meccha Chameleon**, an independent multiplayer game that won four categories: best multiplayer game, best self-published indie, Streamer's Choice and, above all, **Developers' Choice**, the award chosen by developers. A clear sign of how word of mouth on Twitch and YouTube can count for more than budget.

Two awards also went to **Dispatch** (best storytelling and best soundtrack) and two to **CD Projekt Red**: **The Witcher 4** is the most wanted game, while **The Witcher 3 Remastered** wins best remake or remaster.

## All the other winners

- **PC Game of the Year**: Crimson Desert
- **Best Visual Design**: Control Resonant (we've covered [Remedy's sales and finances](/articoli/control-resonant-vendite-remedy/))
- **Best Game Expansion**: Doom: The Dark Ages – Revelations
- **Best Indie Game**: Valheim
- **Best Early Access Game**: Subnautica 2
- **Best Gaming Hardware**: Steam Controller
- **Critics' Choice Award**: Big Walk
- **Best Game Adaptation**: Iron Lung
- **Still Playing Award**: Minecraft (PC and console) and Balatro (mobile)

The full list is on the [official GamesRadar+ website](https://gamesradar.com/games/all-the-golden-joystick-awards-2026-winners-revealed). Attention now shifts to **The Game Awards** in December: Requiem arrives as one of the favourites, but will have to contend with other heavyweights of the year, starting with the **Ocarina of Time** remake.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who won Game of the Year at the Golden Joystick Awards 2026?

Capcom's Resident Evil Requiem, which also won best console game, best trailer and best lead performance.

### How does voting work at the Golden Joystick Awards?

Most categories are voted for by the public on the official website; some awards, such as Critics' Choice, are decided by a jury.

### When did the Golden Joystick Awards 2026 take place?

The ceremony was held in London on Wednesday 11 November 2026.
