No new **Switch 2 price increase**, at least for now. **Nintendo** said so on 5 November, presenting its results for the six months to September: the company has secured enough memory for the coming months and has no plans for further price rises. On the same day, however, the figures showed a slowdown: **Nintendo Switch 2** sales over the half-year fell by 37% compared with last year.

## Switch 2 won't go up in price: what Nintendo said

As reported by VGC, Everyeye and Switch Italia, president **Shuntaro Furukawa** explained that Nintendo has sufficient stocks of components to hold the price steady in the near term. It is an important reassurance, because the underlying problem has not gone away: the RAM shortage that is driving up electronics prices and which, according to VGC, is expected to continue into 2027.

In its investor documents Nintendo puts a figure on the impact of this crisis: the rising cost of components, especially memory, together with tariffs, weighs on the year's forecasts by around 100 billion yen.

## How much Switch 2 costs in Italy today

In Italy Switch 2 has already gone from €469.99 to **€499.99** with the first price rise, introduced precisely because of memory costs. Nintendo is not alone: **Sony** and **Microsoft** have raised the prices of **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S** several times, and today every console of this generation costs more than it did at launch.

One question remains open: the price of physical games. Everyeye reports that a cartridge chip manufacturer has left the industry, and it is not clear whether this will affect boxed editions.

## Switch 2 sales down 37%: is it a problem?

The figures need to be read carefully. In the April-September half-year Nintendo sold **6.49 million** Switch 2 units, 37.4% fewer than in the same period of 2025, the launch period. In the summer quarter alone, units came to 2.68 million, and the total has reached **26.35 million** consoles shipped.

The drop was expected: Nintendo had already forecast lower sales in the second year and is sticking to its estimates. Switch 2 also remains more than six million units ahead of the original Switch and PS5 at the same point in their lifespans, although it is worth remembering that both had availability problems. The fall in hardware sales is reflected in the accounts: according to VGC, profit fell by 16%.

Software is doing better: Switch 2 games sold over the half-year grew by 18.8%, driven by **Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream** and **Mario Kart World**. And this month brought the [Zelda Ocarina of Time remake](/articoli/zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake/), one of the titles Nintendo is counting on for the holidays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Not for now: Nintendo says it has no plans for new price rises because it has enough memory for the coming months. However, this is not a long-term guarantee.

### How much does Switch 2 cost in Italy?

The list price is €499.99 after the increase from the €469.99 launch price. Shops often have deals and bundles at different prices.
