**Path of Exile 2** has a final release date and an almost complete line-up. During **ExileCon 2026**, the **Grinding Gear Games** convention that opened on 6 November, the studio showed the 1.0 version arriving on **11 December**: a new **Shadow** class, the last two acts of the campaign and a league built around sieges and armies.

## When Path of Exile 2 comes out and how much it costs

The 11 December 2026 date (Pacific time, so in the evening in Italy or overnight between the 11th and 12th) had been announced in August at Gamescom and is now confirmed. With 1.0 the game leaves early access, which began in December 2024, and becomes **free-to-play for everyone**: no more entry pack.

The platforms are the ones already known: PC (official website, Steam and Epic Games Store), **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. Anyone who registers on the official website by 10 December will receive cosmetic rewards tied to community milestones.

## What's new in version 1.0: Shadow, Duelist and two new acts

The campaign finally reaches six acts. **Acts 5 and 6** add 24 new areas and 18 new bosses; the fifth is set in Oriath, a place dear to anyone who played the first game.

The star of the presentation is the **Shadow**, a class with daggers and traps built around stealth: killing an enemy recharges the concealment state, and traps placed while hidden only explode when you reveal yourself. It joins the **Duelist**, the sword-wielding warrior shown in the summer, who with the Knight Ascendancy brings along an equippable squire and a war banner.

There is a flip side, though: of the 12 classes promised, 10 will be available, and the last two will be added after launch. Director **Jonathan Rogers** apologised to players, promising that development will continue.

Rounding out the package are a revised passive tree, around 60 new active skills, more than 40 new unique items, 25 new endgame maps and CPU performance that is much improved compared with the start of early access.

## Conquerors of Wraeclast, the league with castles and sieges

The launch league is called **Conquerors of Wraeclast** and it is the most ambitious ever seen in the series: you recruit mercenaries, lead troops in real-time battles and build a stronghold in one of three styles (Ezomyte, Maraketh or Karui), with more than 20 buildings. In the endgame, factions fight over a hundred fortresses on the Atlas map, which grant bonuses to the whole force.

The first game is not forgotten: **Path of Exile** will get the **Covens of Umbra** league on 13 November, with a redesigned Atlas and new loot rules. Among the end-of-year releases, it is one of the PC titles to mark on the calendar, alongside other big 2026 action games such as [Phantom Blade Zero, which launched with record sales](/articoli/phantom-blade-zero-vendite/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the full version of Path of Exile 2 come out?

On 11 December 2026 on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, as it leaves early access.

### Is Path of Exile 2 free?

Yes: with version 1.0 it becomes free-to-play for everyone, with optional purchases limited to cosmetics and convenience items, as in the first game.

### How many classes will Path of Exile 2 have at launch?

Ten of the twelve planned, including the new Shadow and Duelist. The last two will arrive in later updates.
