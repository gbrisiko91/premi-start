**Ocarina of Time**'s **Master Quest** could also return in the remake for **Nintendo Switch 2**, but it is not official yet. Some dataminers have found several references to the "hard" version of the **Nintendo 64** classic in the files of the game, which came out on 5 November, with checks that suggest paid downloadable content. **Nintendo** has not announced anything for now.

## What dataminers found in Ocarina of Time's files

As reported by Multiplayer.it and My Nintendo News, the remake's executable contains several entries linked to the abbreviation "MQ", such as **HasMQ**, **IsMQModeExist**, **NewGameMQ** and **ReleaseMQ**. The references appear alongside the files for dungeons, saves and game completion.

Dataminer **OatmealDome**, who shared the findings on X building on the work of MrCheeze, claims that some of these functions check whether a DLC is owned before unlocking the mode. The files also reportedly contain traces of a mirrored world and modified dungeons, while the map of Hyrule does not seem to change.

It needs to be said clearly: a reference in the code is not an announcement. It could be upcoming content, a discarded idea or a feature that will never see the light of day.

## What Zelda's Master Quest is and why fans want it

Master Quest was created as an alternative version of Ocarina of Time, with the same locations but redesigned dungeons, different puzzles and fiercer enemies. It debuted on **GameCube** between 2002 and 2003, on a bonus disc tied to the release of The Wind Waker, and returned in 2011 in the **Nintendo 3DS** version, which made it even harder with the mirrored world.

The mode was missing at the launch of the Switch 2 remake, and many fans had noticed. In an interview with IGN, producer **Eiji Aonuma** neither confirmed nor denied it: he said the team wants to keep some possibilities open for the future, but that there is nothing specific to announce right now. An answer that, read together with the datamine, fuels hopes.

## Ocarina of Time remake soars in UK sales

Meanwhile, the game is doing well in shops. According to UK physical sales data shared by journalist Christopher Dring and reported by My Nintendo News, the remake debuted at number one and sold around 33% more than **Breath of the Wild** at launch, while remaining below half of **Tears of the Kingdom**. It would be the second-best debut ever for a Zelda game in the country and the biggest physical launch of 2026 so far. Italian figures are not yet available.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Ocarina of Time on Switch 2 include Master Quest?

No, Master Quest is not included at launch. A datamine suggests it could arrive in the future as DLC, but Nintendo has not confirmed it.

### What is Ocarina of Time's Master Quest?

It is an alternative version of the game with redesigned dungeons and puzzles and a higher difficulty. It came out on GameCube and then in the 3DS version, with the mirrored world.

### When is the Ocarina of Time DLC for Switch 2 coming out?

No DLC has been announced yet. For now there are only traces in the game's files and a vague answer from producer Eiji Aonuma.
