**Nagoshi Studio** no longer exists. The studio founded by **Toshihiro Nagoshi**, the creator of the **Yakuza** series (now *Like a Dragon*), was dissolved by resolution of its shareholders with effect from 31 October 2026. Confirmation came on 2 November with a notice in the Kanpo, the Japanese government's official gazette. Along with the studio goes **Gang of Dragon**, its first and only game, which at this point will never see the light of day.

## Why Nagoshi Studio closed

The crisis started with **NetEase**, the Chinese giant that had funded the studio since its founding in 2021. On 9 March 2026 **Bloomberg** revealed that NetEase would stop funding the project from May: completing Gang of Dragon would have required at least another 7 billion yen, around $44 million. A company spokesperson had confirmed the decision.

From then on, the signs were all negative. Nagoshi looked for new investors without finding any; the studio's social media accounts have been silent since February, in May the website and YouTube channel disappeared for a while, and many developers left. Co-founder **Daisuke Sato** himself now describes himself as "formerly of Nagoshi Studio".

The closure is part of NetEase's retreat from investments abroad. The group had already closed **Ouka Studio**, another Japanese team, at the end of 2024, and according to Bloomberg it has cut staff and closed studios around the world.

## What Gang of Dragon was

Gang of Dragon was unveiled with a trailer at **The Game Awards 2025** in December. It was an action game set in Kabukicho, the red-light district of Shinjuku in Tokyo: the same kind of city fans know from the Yakuza games, with bare-knuckle fighting, swords, firearms and car chases.

The protagonist, Shin Ji-seong, is the boss of a Korean criminal organisation, played by actor **Ma Dong-seok** (known in the West as Don Lee). It had been announced for PC on Steam, where the page is still listed as "coming soon", and there was also talk of a PS5 version.

## What happens now to Toshihiro Nagoshi

For now neither Nagoshi nor NetEase has commented on the closure, and it is not known whether the rights to Gang of Dragon could pass to another publisher. Given the estimated cost of finishing it, such a scenario seems unlikely.

For fans it is another blow in a tough year for the industry, after cases such as [EA's closure of BioWare](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/). And it makes the story of one of Japan's most recognisable directors even more bitter: in 2021 he left **SEGA** and Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio to start again from scratch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Gang of Dragon been cancelled?

There is no official cancellation announcement, but the studio developing it was dissolved on 31 October 2026: without a new publisher the game cannot be released.

### Who is Toshihiro Nagoshi?

He is the creator of the Yakuza series and former director of SEGA's Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio, which he left in 2021 to found Nagoshi Studio with backing from NetEase.

### Why did NetEase stop funding Nagoshi Studio?

According to Bloomberg, finishing the game would have required at least 7 billion yen more, while NetEase is reducing its investments in video games outside China.
