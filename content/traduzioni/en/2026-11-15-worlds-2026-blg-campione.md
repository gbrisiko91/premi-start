**Bilibili Gaming** are world champions. Overnight between Saturday and Sunday (Italian time), they beat **Gen.G** 3-1 in the **League of Legends** **Worlds 2026** final at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn. It is BLG's first World Championship and the first for a Chinese team since 2021. It brings to an end four years of Korean titles: DRX in 2022, then T1 in 2023, 2024 and 2025.

## How the Gen.G vs BLG final played out

Gen.G won game 1 in 38 minutes. BLG had controlled the early game and secured the dragon soul, but Gen.G won the fights around Baron and the Elder Dragons and closed out the game. Ruler, on Corki, finished with 7 kills, no deaths and 8 assists.

Game 2 was the longest of the series, at 53 minutes. Gen.G held out against a long BLG siege after a Baron, then gave way. The standout was Xun on Naafiri, with 8 kills, one death and 9 assists.

BLG also won game 3, in 37 minutes, with Viper on Taliyah going 6 kills and 11 assists. In game 4, which lasted 42 minutes, Ruler played well on Xayah too, but Bin on Gwen (8 kills, 2 deaths and 9 assists) carried BLG to the title.

## Knight beats Chovy: the final between two title-less mid laners

Before the final, Hotspawn had dubbed it the clash of the "two princes", because neither Knight nor Chovy had ever won a World Championship. Knight came out on top, two years after losing the final to T1 in London.

Bin, Xun and ON also win their first world title alongside him. For Viper it is the second: he won his first in 2021 with EDward Gaming, the last Chinese team to be champions before BLG.

Chovy ends with the first final of his career, but still without a title. It was also Gen.G's first world final since taking on that name: previously they had stopped four times in the semi-finals (2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025). They arrived in Brooklyn after winning the LCK Cup and the Korean league, and had lost just one game in the whole tournament.

## Bilibili Gaming's 2026

In March BLG had won First Stand by beating G2 3-1, their first international trophy. In July they lost the MSI final 3-2 to Hanwha Life.

At Worlds they got off to a poor start. In the Swiss stage they lost their first match to Movistar KOI and then the one against Top Esports, and only qualified by beating Hanwha Life in the last series. From then on they won every knockout series: 3-0 against Dplus KIA in the quarter-finals, 3-1 against T1 in the semi-finals and 3-1 against Gen.G in the final.

BLG take home $1 million from a $5 million prize pool. Gen.G collect $800,000.
