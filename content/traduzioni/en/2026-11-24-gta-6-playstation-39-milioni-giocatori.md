**GTA 6** had more than 39 million unique players on **PlayStation 5** in its first four days. **Sony Interactive Entertainment** shared the figure in a post on the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/23/thank-you-for-making-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-biggest-launch-in-playstation-history/) published overnight between 23 and 24 November, Italian time: for Sony it is the biggest launch in PlayStation history, across three decades and five console generations.

## GTA 6 on PS5: Sony's numbers

The data is Sony's own and covers the period from 19 November, release day, to 22 November. Over those four days players racked up 683 million hours of play on PS5, an average of 17.4 hours each.

According to the post, the **Rockstar Games** title also set the record for concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, beating multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player games. The launch period also brought the day with the most new PS5 consoles connected online since the console's debut, excluding the end-of-year holidays. GTA 6 was the first game launched on 70% of new PS5s.

## Players, not copies sold

Mind the count: Sony is talking about unique players who started GTA 6 on PS5, not copies sold. The count covers only Sony's console.

For sales you have to look at Take-Two's numbers: on 20 November the company estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales on day one. Before release the game had already passed 32 million copies, as we covered in our piece on [GTA 6 pre-orders](/articoli/gta-6-preordini-take-two/).
