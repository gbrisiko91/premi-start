**Fortnite Crew** is getting a makeover from **5 December**. **Epic Games** announced on 4 November that the monthly **Fortnite** subscription will give more **V-Bucks**, up to 1,600 a month for long-term subscribers, but will stop handing out each month's exclusive skin and will lose the **OG Pass**. It's a change of direction that doesn't please everyone: outfit collectors lose one of the main reasons to subscribe.

## How many V-Bucks Fortnite Crew gives from 5 December

The main new feature is a loyalty-based tier system. The more consecutive months you stay subscribed, the more V-Bucks you get each month:

- **1-2 months**: 1,000 V-Bucks
- **3-5 months**: 1,200 V-Bucks
- **6-11 months**: 1,400 V-Bucks
- **12 months or more**: 1,600 V-Bucks

Existing tenure counts too: anyone who has been subscribed for more than a year goes straight to the top tier. For many it is also a U-turn: in June 2026 Epic had cut the monthly allowance from 1,000 to 800 V-Bucks, a move that went down badly. According to Beebom, the subscription price stays the same.

Subscribers also get a 20% experience points bonus. According to Beebom, a Crew member's teammates in turn receive an extra 10%.

## The end of monthly skins and the OG Pass: what you lose

The December Crew pack will be the **last**: from January there will be no more exclusive outfit every month, which had been the subscription's hallmark since 2020. Epic says anyone who has not yet unlocked all the "Legacy" styles for Crew outfits will receive them automatically.

The OG Pass also leaves the subscription on 5 December. The **Fortnite OG** mode will close with a finale for Chapter 1 Season X, while the next OG era is planned for late 2027. Still included are the **Battle Pass**, the **Music Pass**, the **LEGO Pass** and Rocket League's **Rocket Pass Premium**.

## Is Fortnite Crew still worth subscribing to?

It depends on how you play. For those who spend V-Bucks in the Item Shop or want to unlock the seasonal passes, the new Crew is more generous, especially after a year of subscription. For those who subscribed for the exclusive skins, however, the value drops: those outfits were only available through the Crew and could not be bought any other way.

The change comes at an already eventful time for Fortnite, amid controversy over skin prices and the hunt for the [Pirate Shop Token](/articoli/fortnite-gettone-negozio-pirata/), which has spawned a black market for accounts. The full official announcement is on the [Fortnite website](https://www.fortnite.com/news/big-changes-to-fortnite-crew-launching-december-5).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Fortnite Crew change?

The changes take effect on 5 December 2026. The December Crew pack is the last one with an exclusive skin.

### How many V-Bucks does Fortnite Crew give?

From 1,000 to 1,600 V-Bucks a month, depending on how many consecutive months you stay subscribed.

### Is the Battle Pass still included in Fortnite Crew?

Yes: the Battle Pass, Music Pass, LEGO Pass and Rocket Pass Premium remain included. Only the OG Pass is going.
