**CD Projekt RED** has confirmed that it used artificial intelligence to touch up Geralt's Polish voice in **The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past**, the new expansion expected in 2027. It comes down to the health of long-time voice actor **Jacek Rozenek**: he recorded every line, but his voice changed after a stroke in 2019, and the studio asked for his consent to bring it closer to that of the original game.

The news was broken by Game File and picked up by several outlets, including Multiplayer.it and Push Square.

## How AI works on Geralt's voice in Songs of the Past

The work was entrusted to Respeecher, a company specialising in synthetic voices. Rozenek recorded all the new lines as normal; then the Songs of the Past recordings and the original The Witcher 3 ones were used to train a model on his voice as it was back then. The model is then used to "transform" the new recordings so that they sound like the Geralt of 2015.

According to Multiplayer.it, the training and the correction of AI-generated flaws took dozens of hours of work. The English version stays as it is: Doug Cockle returns as Geralt without any intervention.

## Jacek Rozenek's consent and CD Projekt's position

Rozenek, who after the stroke had for a time lost the ability to speak and returned to voice acting in 2022, gave his approval. His aim, he explained, is for the voice to be "as close as possible to the one from twelve years ago".

Localisation producer Mikołaj Szwed acknowledged the ethical concerns around AI, but called this a responsible use, made possible only by the actor's consent and motivated solely by his health. The studio had been here before: with Respeecher it had recreated Viktor Vektor's Polish voice in Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty, after the actor's death and with his family's permission.

## Why AI in video games is still divisive

Between voice actor strikes, stores flooded with generated games and controversies over AI-created assets, every announcement of this kind sparks debate. A recent example is [itch.io's crackdown on AI-made games](/articoli/itch-io-giochi-ia-eliminati/).

This time, though, fans have mostly reacted well, because the AI restores the performance of an actor who recorded every line and agrees with it, without inventing a new one. Where to draw the line remains an open question all the same. For The Witcher 4, on the other hand, the issue should not arise: Rozenek's current voice would suit an older Geralt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does The Witcher 3 Songs of the Past come out?

The expansion is expected in 2027; CD Projekt RED has not yet announced an exact date.

### Does Geralt have an AI-generated voice in Songs of the Past?

No: Jacek Rozenek recorded all the Polish lines and AI was only used to bring his timbre closer to that of 2015. In English the voice remains Doug Cockle's.

### Does the Italian dub of The Witcher 3 change?

The Witcher 3 in Italian only has subtitles, so the news concerns the Polish version and not those who play with English audio.
