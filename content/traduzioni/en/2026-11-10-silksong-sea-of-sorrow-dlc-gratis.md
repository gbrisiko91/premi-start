**Hollow Knight: Silksong** will get more than one free expansion. The news doesn't come from a **Team Cherry** announcement, but from the instruction booklet of the physical edition, currently being distributed on **PS5**: one page describes **Sea of Sorrow** as the game's "first major expansion" and promises further free content packs. The detail, shared on X on 9 November by a user, has been picked up by several international outlets.

## What the Silksong manual says about expansions

The booklet's text is short but clear. Silksong, it reads, will grow with **free content packs** bringing new adventures, new allies, new abilities and new enemies. Sea of Sorrow is described as "coming soon" and, above all, as the first of a series.

It is the first time Team Cherry has put in writing a plan that goes beyond a single expansion. This won't come as much of a surprise to anyone familiar with the Australian studio: the original **Hollow Knight** received no fewer than four free updates after release, including Godmaster and The Grimm Troupe, which extended its life for years.

## When is Silksong Sea of Sorrow released

Here comes the sore point. Sea of Sorrow was announced at the end of 2025 as a free sea-themed expansion, with new adventures for **Hornet**, and with a release planned for 2026. At the time Team Cherry promised to share more information shortly before launch.

With less than two months left in the year, however, there is still no date, and the manual merely offers a generic "coming soon". If the expansion doesn't come out by December, it will effectively slip to 2027. The studio, known for communicating very little, has not commented.

## Silksong's physical edition: what's inside

The physical edition is produced by **Fangamer** and is available for PS5, **Xbox Series X|S** and **Switch 2**. The box contains the game, a reversible cover, a 32-page manual and a small poster. Sea of Sorrow is not on the disc: when it comes out it will need to be downloaded, free of charge just as for owners of the digital version.

A printed manual revealing a developer's plans is a rarity these days, which is partly why the news has spread so widely among fans. The game, after all, is still going strong: on Steam it has over 90% positive reviews more than a year after release. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sea of Sorrow free?

Yes. Team Cherry has confirmed that Sea of Sorrow will be a free expansion for all owners of Hollow Knight: Silksong.

### When is Sea of Sorrow released?

There is no date yet. Team Cherry had indicated 2026, but the physical edition's manual only says "coming soon".

### Will Silksong get more DLC?

According to the physical edition's manual, yes: after Sea of Sorrow, further free content packs are planned, still without names or dates.
