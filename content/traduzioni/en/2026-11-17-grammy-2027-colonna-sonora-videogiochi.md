On 16 November the Recording Academy announced the nominations for the **2027 Grammys**, and among the 100 categories is the one dedicated to **video game** music: Best Score Soundtrack for Video Games and Other Interactive Media. Five soundtracks are in contention, including those for **Ghost of Yotei** and 007 First Light. The winners will be announced on Sunday 7 February 2027.

## The video game soundtracks nominated for the 2027 Grammys

Here are the titles in the running, with the composers listed by the Recording Academy:

- *007 First Light*: Joe Henson and Alexis Leon Smith, the duo The Flight
- *Ghost of Yotei*: Toma Otowa
- *Marvel's Wolverine*: David Fleming
- *Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora - From the Ashes*: John Paesano
- *Star Wars Zero Company*: Gordy Haab

Two of the five nominations come from PlayStation Studios games: Sucker Punch's Ghost of Yotei and Insomniac Games' Marvel's Wolverine.

## Who is eligible for the 2027 Grammys

For this edition the Recording Academy is considering recordings released between 31 August 2025 and 28 August 2026. What counts, therefore, is the release of the soundtrack album, not necessarily that of the game. The final vote by Academy members will run from 10 December 2026 to 7 January 2027.

Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora earns its third nomination in a row in the category: in 2025 with the base game, in 2026 with the Secrets of the Spires expansion and now with From the Ashes. Gordy Haab already knows the award: in 2024 he won it together with Stephen Barton for Star Wars Jedi: Survivor.

The award for video game soundtracks was introduced at the 2023 Grammys. At the last edition it went to Austin Wintory for *Sword of the Sea*.
