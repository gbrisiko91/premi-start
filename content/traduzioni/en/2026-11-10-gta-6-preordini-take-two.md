Nine days before release, **GTA 6 pre-orders** are going better than even **Take-Two Interactive** expected. CEO **Strauss Zelnick** said so on 9 November while presenting the quarterly results: reservations for **Grand Theft Auto VI** continue to reach, in his words, "unprecedented levels". The **Rockstar Games** title arrives on 19 November on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**.

On the same day the publisher confirmed that **GTA 5** has reached around 235 million copies sold, and Rockstar announced a live music festival in Miami to celebrate the launch.

## How many pre-orders does GTA 6 have: what we actually know

Take-Two has not released an official pre-order figure. Zelnick, also interviewed by Variety, preferred to stay cautious: the company never declares a success before it arrives, and the numbers, he explained, will speak for themselves in a few days.

The figures doing the rounds are therefore **third-party estimates**. According to analytics firm Sensor Tower, cited by several outlets, digital pre-orders stand at around 7.7 million, worth close to $750 million, with PS5 well ahead of Xbox. Again according to these estimates, most players have opted for the most expensive edition, the $100 Ultimate. These numbers should be treated with caution until Take-Two confirms them.

## Take-Two: a weaker quarter, but GTA 6's year remains record-breaking

The paradox is that the quarter just ended was weak. According to [Take-Two's official press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm), net bookings fell by 18%, from $1.96 billion to $1.6 billion, with a net loss of $54 million.

Investors, however, are looking ahead. Take-Two confirmed its forecast for the full fiscal year, which ends in March 2027: between **$8 billion and $8.2 billion** in net bookings. For the October–December quarter alone, the one in which GTA 6 launches, the company expects between $3.35 billion and $3.5 billion, more than double the previous quarter.

Meanwhile GTA 5 shows no sign of stopping: thirteen years after release it still sells around five million copies a quarter and stands at **235 million**, the second best-selling game of all time after Minecraft.

## GTA 6 release and preload: the dates to remember

The schedule for the coming days is packed:

- **10 November**: digital preload begins on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. All the details are in our article on [how big GTA 6 is and how much space to free up](/articoli/gta-6-preload-peso/);
- **12 November**: physical editions with a download code arrive in shops;
- **19 November**: GTA 6 unlocks worldwide;
- **21 November**: **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival** takes place in Miami, a concert organised by Rockstar with PlayStation at Mana Wynwood, featuring Travis Scott, Cardi B, Metro Boomin and Justice, who worked on the game's soundtrack. Tickets are allocated through a lottery that closes today, 10 November, at 10pm Italian time.

As for the PC version, Take-Two had nothing new to say: any announcements, Zelnick reiterated, are up to Rockstar.
