Today, 7 November, is **N7 Day**, the day that celebrates **Mass Effect** every year (the date is a nod to Commander Shepard's N7 rank). The 2026 edition, however, is unlike any before it: it is the first without **BioWare**, which **Electronic Arts** shut down on 21 October along with the cancellation of the new instalment. Fans have turned the celebration into a protest, while the official store keeps selling merchandise.

## What fans are doing for Mass Effect's N7 Day

The **We Are Mass Effect** community has asked everyone to play **Mass Effect Legendary Edition** today between 8pm and 11pm Italian time (2-5pm US Eastern), sharing screenshots and clips with the hashtag **#SaveMassEffect**. The goal is to show EA, with hard numbers, that the series still has a living audience.

The protest is physical too. On 6 November a themed advertising truck drove around EA's headquarters in **Redwood City**, California, for about four hours, staying on public roads. At the end of October a digital billboard had already appeared in Times Square, and a fundraiser passed 5,000 dollars in under twenty minutes to pay for more billboards, including during **The Game Awards** in December. Any surplus will go to the laid-off developers.

## The Save Mass Effect petition passes 300,000 signatures

The online petition launched after the closure has passed **300,000 signatures**, according to the organisers. In Canada, where BioWare was based, a second petition has been submitted to the House of Commons: it asks the government to consider financial support so that former developers can buy the studio and its brands from EA. It has gathered the signatures needed to be considered, but the outcome is far from certain.

We covered the background here: according to Bloomberg, [EA reportedly shut down BioWare to focus on Star Wars Battlefront 3](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/), an account never confirmed by the publisher.

## The N7 Day merchandise angering fans

What is irritating the community is the **BioWare Gear Store**, which is still running: ahead of N7 Day it opened pre-orders for a wearable N7 Breather helmet from Mass Effect 3 with LEDs that switch from Paragon blue to Renegade red, as well as T-shirts, pins and a board game. Many saw it as an attempt to monetise a series that has just been put on hold.

**Dark Horse** teased a new statue and **Youtooz** a line of collectibles. Asked by a fan on X, Dark Horse replied that the announcement had been planned long before the closure and that it still wanted to give fans something to look forward to. EA has made no public comment on the merchandise.

## What to expect for the future of Mass Effect

To date, EA has not announced any plans to resume the project or hand the series to another studio. What remains are the rights to the brand, the documentary *Massive Effect: The BioWare Story* due in 2027 and the TV series announced in the past, whose fate is uncertain. Whether N7 Day has any effect, we will find out in the coming months.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Mass Effect's N7 Day?

It is the day dedicated to Mass Effect, celebrated every 7 November: the name refers to N7, Commander Shepard's military designation.

### Has BioWare really been shut down?

Yes. EA announced the closure of the studio on 21 October 2026, cancelling the new Mass Effect, with around 400 jobs cut.

### How can I take part in N7 Day 2026?

By playing Mass Effect Legendary Edition today between 8pm and 11pm Italian time and sharing your sessions with the hashtag #SaveMassEffect.
