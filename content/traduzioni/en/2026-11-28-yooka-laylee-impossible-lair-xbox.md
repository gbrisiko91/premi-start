**Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition** will be released on 15 December on PS5, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC (Steam), but not on Xbox. In a message on X picked up on Friday 27 November by Pure Xbox, **Playtonic Games** explained why: bringing the game to other platforms currently costs too much for the British studio.

The 2019 2.5D platformer is therefore getting an improved version on only three platforms, while Xbox players are left with the original Xbox One edition.

## What changes in the Definitive Edition

Playtonic presented the new edition on 24 November on its website, with graphical improvements and higher performance. According to the details reported by Gematsu, on PS5 the game runs at 4K and 120 frames per second, with improved lighting and shadows. On Switch 2 the resolution goes up, both on TV and in handheld mode, and there is a 120 fps mode. The PC version adds an "Ultra" graphics setting.

The upgrade is free for those who already own the game digitally: the official announcement only mentions owners of the digital version and says nothing about physical copies. The Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack add-on is included.

## Why Yooka-Laylee is not coming to Xbox

In the message Playtonic writes that the cost of bringing the game to more platforms "is not sustainable at this time" and that it cannot commit to other versions in the near future. So the studio does not rule out an Xbox edition, but it does not promise one either.

On Series X|S backward compatibility remains: according to Digital Foundry's John Linneman, quoted by Pure Xbox, the Xbox One X version already runs in native 4K. Microsoft has also made progress on backward compatibility on PC, with the arrival of [original Xbox games such as KOTOR and Morrowind](/articoli/giochi-xbox-originali-pc/).
