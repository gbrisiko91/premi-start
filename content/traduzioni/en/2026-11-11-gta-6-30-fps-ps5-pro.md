It is now official: **GTA 6** will run at **30 fps** on consoles. **Rockstar Games** confirmed it on 10 November, the day preloading began, explaining that **Grand Theft Auto VI** was built from the ground up targeting 30 frames per second on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. The only console getting something extra is **PS5 Pro**, with PSSR upscaling and exclusive ray-traced shadows.

Until yesterday the 30 fps cap was just a rumour, which started in August from an interview with a developer. Now it comes from Rockstar itself, nine days before the 19 November release.

## GTA 6 at 30 fps: what Rockstar said

According to VGC and Multiplayer.it, Rockstar explained that the game is designed to deliver the best possible experience on the current generation of consoles, and that the target on both platforms is 30 frames per second.

What is missing is just as important: Rockstar **did not mention a performance mode** at 60 fps, nor any alternative graphics options. It has not said whether they will arrive in future updates, or whether there will be a 40 fps middle ground on 120 Hz TVs, as many players hope.

## What changes on PS5 Pro: PSSR and ray-traced shadows

On **PS5 Pro**, GTA 6 will use **PSSR** (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution), Sony's upscaling system, for a sharper image. On top of that, the high-end console is the only one to support **ray-traced shadows**.

The frame rate, however, stays the same: even on the Pro the stated target is 30 fps. Technical analysts had predicted this for months, because the bottleneck in such a dense open world would mainly be the processor, which PS5 Pro does not substantially improve over the base PS5. On the [official PlayStation page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/GTAVIfeatures/) the game is listed as "PS5 Pro Enhanced".

On standard PS5, Sony is instead focusing on the **DualSense**: a new trailer shows haptic feedback that lets you feel weapon recoil in the triggers, resistance while lifting weights and the police radio coming out of the controller's speaker.

## GTA 6 on Xbox One and Series S: cloud gaming

There is also a surprise for those who have not upgraded their console yet. Through **Xbox Cloud Gaming**, GTA 6 will also be playable on **Xbox One**, with the same graphics settings as the **Xbox Series S** version. The game will run on Microsoft's servers, so you will need a stable connection and will have to accept the usual limitations of streaming, such as latency.

Rockstar also confirmed **Remote Play** support on PlayStation and Xbox.

## Why everyone is talking about it

GTA 6 is the most anticipated game in years, and 30 fps is dividing the community: some point out that GTA 5 on PS3 also ran that way and that the game world is what matters, while others consider 60 fps a non-negotiable standard in 2026. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will GTA 6 have a 60 fps mode?

Not for now: Rockstar has only confirmed the 30 fps target on all consoles and has not announced any performance mode, either at launch or in the future.

### Does GTA 6 run better on PS5 Pro?

Yes, but only visually: PS5 Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, while the frame rate stays at 30 fps.

### Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Yes, through Xbox Cloud Gaming, via streaming and with the graphics quality of the Xbox Series S version.
