On 17 November Microsoft revealed the second November wave of **Xbox Game Pass**. Ten games arrive between 17 November and 1 December, with **High on Life 2** and the follow-up to Call of the Sea already available from today, 18 November. On 30 November, meanwhile, eight titles leave the catalogue.

## Xbox Game Pass November 2026: dates and incoming games

All the games on the list are included in Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass. Where indicated, they also come to Game Pass Premium.

- Paw Patrol: Dino World, from 17 November (also Premium)
- Call of the Elder Gods, from 18 November (also Premium)
- High on Life 2, from 18 November (also Premium)
- Roadside Research in Game Preview, from 18 November (also Premium)
- Black Jacket, from 19 November (also Premium)
- Motorslice, from 24 November (also Premium)
- Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2, from 24 November (also Premium)
- Arkheron in Game Preview, from 27 November
- The Outlast Trials, from 1 December (also Premium)
- Hela: of Mice & Magic, from 1 December

## High on Life 2 and Call of the Elder Gods, today's releases

High on Life 2 picks up the formula of the shooter with talking alien guns, this time with skating elements and an intergalactic conspiracy to foil. Call of the Elder Gods is a Lovecraft-inspired narrative puzzle adventure and follows Call of the Sea, released in 2020.

For those looking for something smaller there is Black Jacket, a "purgatorial" take on blackjack with a dark painterly style, from Mi'pu'mi Games and Skystone Games (the team behind Tiny Bookshop).

## The end-of-month arrivals: The Outlast Trials and Hela

On 1 December it is the turn of The Outlast Trials, the horror game in which you act as guinea pigs for the Murkoff Corporation's mind-control experiments. The same day sees the debut of Hela: of Mice & Magic, a 3D co-op adventure where you play as a little mouse among landscapes inspired by northern Sweden: Microsoft lists it as available on Game Pass from launch day, like the PvP shooter Arkheron.

## The games leaving Game Pass on 30 November

Last days for Aliens: Dark Descent, Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden, DreamWorks Gabby's Dollhouse: Ready to Party, Generation Zero, Kill It With Fire! 2, Moving Out 2, Star Trucker and Totally Reliable Delivery Service.
