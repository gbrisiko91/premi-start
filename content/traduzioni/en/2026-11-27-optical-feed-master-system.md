The **Sega Master System** has a new 3D first-person shooter. It's called **Optical Feed**, it's being developed by the independent group **CrispyCathode**, and it has been making the rounds since 25 November thanks to Time Extension, which flagged the demo published on the developer's website.

It's a homebrew game, meaning it has been made today by enthusiasts for a console released in the 1980s, and for now it's a work in progress: the demo mainly serves to show off the 3D movement, which according to Time Extension is clean and smooth.

## What Optical Feed is like on the Master System

The player, armed with a cannon, moves through an isolated, maze-like environment. The enemies resemble the interference on an old television set, an idea that gives the whole thing an unsettling feel, with a few horror elements. The engine uses raycasting, the same technique as the first first-person shooters on PC.

Time Extension compares the graphical and sound style to Castle Master and Total Eclipse, the 3D adventures by Incentive released between 1988 and 1990. Be warned, though: the demo video contains repetitive patterns and flashing images, so anyone sensitive to these effects would do better to avoid it.

## Where to play the Optical Feed demo

The demo is on CrispyCathode's official page, where you can download the ROM, to be used with a Master System emulator or with a flash cart on the original console.

There is no date yet for the full version. The Master System isn't the only vintage console with new games on the way: the Nintendo 64 is getting its own first-person horror too, [Vasalius: Human Error, featuring the voice of Duke Nukem](/articoli/vasalius-human-error-n64/).
