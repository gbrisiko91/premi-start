**Mass Effect 3's multiplayer** is back, and not thanks to **Electronic Arts**. On 7 November, to mark N7 Day, the modding group **ME3Tweaks** released its mod for **Mass Effect Legendary Edition** on PC for free on Nexus Mods: it brings back the online co-op that the 2021 collection had cut, with new modes and community-run servers.

## How the Mass Effect 3 multiplayer mod for Legendary Edition works

The project, which started out under the codename **Project Milan**, is not a simple transplant of the old code. According to the team, it is a genuine remaster of Mass Effect 3's wave-based mode: you fight in squads of up to four players against Cerberus, the Geth, the Reapers and the Collectors, on the maps many remember from 2012.

The main new features, as reported by Kotaku and Nerdly:

- two new match types: **Coalition**, which changes the enemy faction every wave, and **Unified**, which mixes them all together;
- controller support, adjustable field of view, and options for aim assist and heavy weapons;
- redesigned menus, with player ping and downed teammates clearly displayed;
- reworked lighting, improved textures and maps with small touch-ups (on Hazard Giant, for example, there are torches);
- less tiring progression, with options to reduce the "grinding" for unlocks.

Above all, the mod **does not depend on BioWare's servers**: anyone can connect to ME3Tweaks' servers or host a private one for friends. The multiplayer installation stays separate from single-player campaign saves and mods.

## Three years of work and timing that makes noise

The team worked on it on and off for around three years: the first working prototype dates back to December 2023. One of the modders, Mgamerz, told Kotaku that the project started almost as a joke, with the idea of "doing something stupid" and bringing over the multiplayer. The group is also keen to point out that almost everything was done by hand, without artificial intelligence tools.

The release comes at a delicate moment. At the end of October EA closed BioWare and cancelled the new instalment, as we reported when covering the [closure of BioWare and the bet on Battlefront 3](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/). For many fans, who turned [N7 Day 2026 into a protest](/articoli/n7-day-mass-effect-2026/), this mod is the gift the publisher never gave. Among the most discussed details are the new store banners, which use images from the cancelled **Mass Effect**.

According to Everyeye, around 3,000 players downloaded the mod in the first few hours.

## What comes after launch

ME3Tweaks has already listed its next steps: the return of the rebuilt Redemption map pack, the Rejection enemy faction, a store with direct equipment purchases, lobbies of configurable size and a new challenges screen. The team also wants to create a stable base for future mods. There are no dates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Mass Effect Legendary Edition have multiplayer?

Not officially: the 2021 collection does not include Mass Effect 3's co-op mode. On PC it can be added with the free ME3Tweaks mod, released on 7 November 2026.

### Does the Mass Effect multiplayer mod work on PS5 and Xbox?

No, it is PC only: you need a copy of Mass Effect Legendary Edition on computer. Mods cannot be installed on consoles.

### Where can I download the Mass Effect 3 multiplayer mod?

It is free on Nexus Mods, in the section dedicated to Mass Effect Legendary Edition. Instructions are on the ME3Tweaks development blog.
