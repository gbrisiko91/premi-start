**BioWare** has been shut down by **Electronic Arts** and the new **Mass Effect** has been cancelled: that has been official since 21-22 October. Now a Bloomberg report tries to explain why. According to anonymous sources, EA chose to invest in **Star Wars Battlefront 3** rather than the fifth instalment of the saga, and the Canadian studio ended up being sacrificed. EA has not confirmed this account.

## Why EA shut down BioWare, according to Bloomberg

The report describes a review of EA's projects following the company's sale to the Saudi sovereign wealth fund, a deal that reportedly left around **$18 billion of debt**. Against that backdrop, management allegedly had two very expensive science-fiction games on the table, each costing hundreds of millions of dollars:

- the new Mass Effect, which according to the sources would have been called *Mass Effect Universe*;
- a new Star Wars Battlefront.

The choice reportedly fell on the latter, seen as a safer bet. Other factors cited include the cooler reception of BioWare's latest games compared with its classics, as well as some internal dynamics within the company.

## What is confirmed and what isn't

It is confirmed that EA has shut down BioWare, cancelled the new Mass Effect and announced cuts of around **400 jobs**. In a statement to Bloomberg, the publisher spoke of a decision taken after months of planning and analysis, without going into detail.

What is not confirmed is the direct link to Battlefront 3. Variety reported that EA told staff that **DICE** and **Respawn** will work on a "future project": many believe it is the new Battlefront, but there is no official announcement yet.

## Mass Effect cancelled: how fans reacted

The "Save Mass Effect" petition has passed **200,000 signatures**. An alleged Mass Effect Universe trailer has circulated online, and a Grammy-winning composer has published music written for the fifth instalment. According to the latest reports, the Mass Effect TV series is still in the works.

BioWare is the studio behind Baldur's Gate, Knights of the Old Republic, Dragon Age and the Mass Effect trilogy: for many players its closure marks the end of an era, like the end of other historic studios that changed the way we play, from the id Software of [DOOM and Deathmatch](/articoli/doom-deathmatch-romero-petersen-carmack/) onwards. If you want to catch up on the saga, the remastered trilogy is available as [Mass Effect Legendary Edition on the EA website](https://ea.com/sv/games/mass-effect/mass-effect-legendary-edition/buy).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has BioWare really closed?

Yes. EA announced BioWare's closure between 21 and 22 October 2026, together with the cancellation of the new Mass Effect and around 400 layoffs.

### Will Mass Effect 5 be released?

Not at the moment: the project has been cancelled. The remastered trilogy remains available and, according to the latest reports, the TV series is still going ahead.

### Has Star Wars Battlefront 3 been announced?

Not yet. EA has only spoken of a future project handed to DICE and Respawn, which several sources say is Battlefront 3.
