**GTA 6** in Japan is not the same game the rest of us are playing. On launch day, 19 November, Japanese players noticed that **Rockstar Games**' local version has less blood and less nudity, and the editorial team at **GameSpark** compared the two editions on Xbox Series X|S to find out what really changes.

## What changes in the Japanese version of GTA 6

The most obvious sign is in the settings. In the display tab of the international edition there is an option to adjust blood and gore intensity across three levels, with high as the default. It is missing from the Japanese version, which has 11 entries instead of 12. The dismemberment option, on the other hand, remains.

In-game there is still blood, on clothes and on the ground, but less of it stays on the tarmac. Headshots are also more restrained: in the Japanese version heads can still come off, but without the splatters of brain matter that appear abroad with the setting at maximum. According to GameSpark, this is because in Japan the gore level is locked to the lowest setting.

## Jason in the shower, but in his underwear

Then there is the shower, which has featured in many comparison videos posted by players. In the first-person scene Jason keeps his underwear on, while in the international version he is naked. GameSpark thinks it likely that other scenes have been altered in the same way, as had already happened with the Japanese edition of GTA 5.

Kotaku adds that nudity in sex scenes and on the beaches has been covered up with clothing, and that Rockstar had not announced these changes before launch: players discovered them on their own. In Japan the game still carries the highest rating, for ages 18 and over.
