**T1** beat **Karmine Corp** 3-1 in the last quarterfinal of **League of Legends** **Worlds 2026**, played overnight in Allen, Texas. The reigning champions, chasing a fourth consecutive world title that no one has ever won, take on **Bilibili Gaming** in the semifinal on Sunday. KC's exit also means the last European team is gone: the LEC ends the World Championship with no one in the top four.

## How T1 eliminated Karmine Corp at Worlds 2026

The first two games were one-sided. In game 1, T1 kept pace in the early minutes and then took control from the mid game, closing it out in 35 minutes with a composition built to scale. Game 2 lasted less than half an hour, with **Peyz** on Kai'Sa and **Oner** on Trundle dominating the map.

Karmine Corp responded in game 3 with a slow, disciplined game, won thanks to more Barons and an Elder Dragon. Game 4 was the tensest: 35 minutes decided by a play from **Keria**, who closed out the series with Flash and his ultimate. On the other side, **Canna** on Renekton was KC's best player. The match had a special flavour for him: he came through T1's academy and played for the main roster in 2020 and 2021.

For Karmine Corp, the summer's upset was not repeated. In July, at the Esports World Cup in Paris, they had beaten T1 2-1 in the semifinal. Over a best-of-five, however, **Faker** and his teammates made their experience count.

## Worlds 2026 quarterfinal results

| Quarterfinal | Result | Into the semifinals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top Esports vs Invictus Gaming | 1-3 | Invictus Gaming |
| Gen.G vs LYON | 3-0 | Gen.G |
| Dplus KIA vs Bilibili Gaming | 0-3 | Bilibili Gaming |
| T1 vs Karmine Corp | 3-1 | T1 |

**Dplus KIA's collapse.** The biggest surprise of the quarterfinals. DK had finished the Swiss stage without losing a game and went out without winning one. BLG took 14 dragons out of 15 and all four Barons, with 62 kills to 28. In July it was DK who had knocked BLG out of the Esports World Cup: revenge served.

**Gen.G, the perfect machine.** Unbeaten in the Swiss stage, a clean 3-0 against LYON, North America's last hope. For Chovy, Canyon and Ruler it is a third consecutive Worlds semifinal.

**IG's return.** Invictus Gaming, TheShy's team, had not reached the knockout stage in seven years. In the Chinese derby they eliminated Top Esports.

The semifinals are therefore two against two: Korea versus China. Besides DK, Korea has already lost Hanwha Life Esports, the MSI champions, eliminated in the Swiss stage.

## T1 vs Bilibili Gaming: a rematch of the 2024 final

Sunday's semifinal is the most anticipated clash of the tournament. The two teams have already fought for a world title: in the 2024 final in London, T1 won 3-2 at the end of one of the hardest-fought series in the game's history. BLG arrive in form: after a shaky Swiss stage they beat Hanwha Life and then swept DK, and they have never lost a quarterfinal at Worlds.

There is only one question now: can China get two teams into the final and end the Korean era, or will T1 write another chapter of history?
