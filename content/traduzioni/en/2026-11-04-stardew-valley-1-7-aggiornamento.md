**Stardew Valley 1.7** will be the biggest update in the game's history. So said **Eric Barone**, the creator known as **ConcernedApe**, taking the stage at a concert dedicated to his most famous farm. He also gave a hint on timing: the update should probably arrive "next year", meaning 2027.

## What ConcernedApe said about Stardew Valley 1.7

Barone's words come from **Stardew Valley: Symphony of Seasons**, the concert tour featuring the game's music, during its stop at the **Red Rocks Amphitheatre** in Colorado. A fan in the audience uploaded a video of his speech to Reddit, and from there the news made its way around outlets across the globe.

Barone confirmed he is still working on the update and explained that the package will be richer than previous ones, including the gigantic **1.6** from 2024. He described the update as packed with content and closed with a promise to fans that a real surprise awaits them.

## What's already confirmed in update 1.7

Some of 1.7's new features had been known for a while and are the ones the community has asked for most:

- **Clint and Sandy become marriageable**: the village blacksmith and the Calico Desert trader finally join the characters you can court;
- **a new farm type**, adding to the maps already available;
- **more social features**, not yet described in detail.

According to ConcernedApe, though, Clint and Sandy are just the tip of the iceberg. Everything else is still secret, and fans are already speculating about new areas, festivals and characters. For now it's only speculation: none of this content has been announced.

## When Stardew Valley 1.7 comes out and on which platforms

There's no date. ConcernedApe spoke of a likely release sometime next year, without committing any further: that's his style, given that he works almost alone and prefers not to set deadlines. Version 1.6 also took longer than first expected. It's not the only anticipated project to slip into 2027: the same happened to a far bigger title, [FromSoftware's The Duskbloods](/articoli/the-duskbloods-rinvio-2027/).

Like the previous ones, 1.7 should also be **free** and arrive first on **PC** before reaching consoles and smartphones, but Barone hasn't confirmed this. Meanwhile, he is also still working on his next game, **Haunted Chocolatier**.

## Why the new Stardew Valley update is making headlines

Ten years after its release, **Stardew Valley** remains one of the most loved and best-selling indie games, Italy included. Every free update brings millions of people back to the game: after 1.6 it was once again among the most played games on Steam. The idea of an even bigger update, at no cost, is enough on its own to send social media into a frenzy.
