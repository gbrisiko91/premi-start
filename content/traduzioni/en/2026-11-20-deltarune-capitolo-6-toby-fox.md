**Toby Fox** published the autumn **Deltarune** newsletter overnight on 20 November. Chapter 6 already has a complete draft that can be played from start to finish. There is no date yet, though, and the Undertale creator is managing expectations.

## Deltarune Chapter 6: what's left to do

According to Fox, Chapter 6 is structurally complete. The maps, events and battles are all in place, with just one optional event taking longer than expected. Some important scenes and various details are still "underbaked" in their presentation, however, so proper testing will take a little longer.

Translation into other languages and early testing should already be under way. "I won't be able to rush," Fox writes. The next update will come once the Chapter 6 localisation is finished.

## Chapter 7 has only just begun

There is very little of Chapter 7 so far. Fox read the team its events and dialogue, but stopped at a crucial moment and kept the rest to himself until the game is further along. Those who have finished their work on Chapter 6 have already moved on to the next one. Of Chapter 7, Fox writes: "This time I really feel like I know what I'm doing."

The team has grown since the days of Chapter 4, but the newsletter explains that it still takes more and more time to keep up the fastest possible pace.

## The rest of the newsletter

Queen won the poll between Queen and Pluey with 65% of the vote, and the prize is a Chapter 6 scene shown on video. Fox also introduces Apple Girlington, a character cut from Chapter 5 and drawn by his friend Marina, and a new collection of piano pieces by Trevor Alan Gomes, volume 2.

No prices or platforms were given for the new chapters. Meanwhile, on the indie front, anyone waiting for more news can read what [Silksong's physical manual revealed about upcoming free DLC](/articoli/silksong-sea-of-sorrow-dlc-gratis/).
