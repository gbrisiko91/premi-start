**PS5** has passed **100 million consoles sold**. That is the word from **Sony Interactive Entertainment**, which has updated its official sales data page: the threshold was crossed as of 10 November 2026, just days before the console's sixth birthday, having launched on 12 November 2020 in North America and Japan and on 19 November in Europe.

## PS5 at 100 million: Sony's official numbers

On [Sony's sales data page](https://sonyinteractive.com/en/our-company/business-data-sales/) the PS5 now appears with the wording "more than 100 million" units shipped worldwide. These are consoles delivered to retailers (so-called sell-in), not necessarily already in players' homes.

According to Push Square, since the end of September Sony has shipped around **2.1 million consoles** in just over a month: a strong pace, driven by the arrival of the holiday season and, in all likelihood, by anticipation for **GTA 6**. That last link, however, is speculation by the outlets, not a figure confirmed by Sony.

## How it compares with PS4, PS2 and the original PlayStation

The PS5 is Sony's fourth home console to pass the 100 million mark. Here are the official totals listed on the same page:

- **PlayStation 2**: over 160 million (as of 2012), still the best-selling console of all time;
- **PlayStation 4**: over 117 million (as of 2022);
- **PlayStation** (the original): over 102.4 million;
- **PlayStation 5**: over 100 million (as of 10 November 2026).

The PS4 remains the fastest, though: it had reached 100 million in summer 2019, around five and a half years after launch. The PS5 took a few months longer, partly because of the stock shortages of its first two years, during the pandemic, and the hardware price rises of the years that followed. At the current pace, overtaking the original PlayStation is a matter of weeks.

## Why the PS5 milestone matters

A hundred million is a symbolic threshold, but also a commercial one: more consoles mean more potential buyers for every game. And that is exactly what **Rockstar Games** and **Take-Two** need ahead of the launch of GTA 6, whose [record pre-order numbers](/articoli/gta-6-preordini-take-two/) we've reported on.

For Sony it is also an answer to those who spoke of a struggling generation: according to the company, the PS5 generation is already the most profitable PlayStation generation ever. It remains to be seen how much room is left before the arrival of **PS6**, which many analysts expect within the next two years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many PS5s have been sold?

According to Sony, as of 10 November 2026 more than 100 million PS5s have been shipped worldwide, across all models.

### What is the best-selling PlayStation console?

The PlayStation 2, with over 160 million units. It is followed by PS4 (over 117 million), the original PlayStation (over 102.4 million) and now PS5.

### Has PS5 sold more than PS4?

Not yet: PS4 has passed 117 million and reached 100 million faster than PS5.
