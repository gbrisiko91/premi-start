The **Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time remake** could have an unexpected backer. A fan, who has not revealed their name, has written an open letter to **Ubisoft** offering **over €5 million** to resume and complete the project, which was cancelled in January 2026. The offer has spread from X and Reddit to outlets around the world, but Ubisoft has not responded so far.

## What the fan is offering for the Prince of Persia remake

In the letter, shared by the fan account PoP Universe on X and then discussed on Reddit, the fan mentions an initial budget of more than €5 million, around $5.6 million. They also say they are ready to put more money on the table, but only after a formal agreement, a feasibility check on the project and whatever conditions Ubisoft considers fair.

The most surprising part concerns what they ask for in return: nothing. The fan claims not to want profits, a share of sales or any rights to the intellectual property. Their only aim, they write, is "to help this video game reach players". They also ask whether there is an official channel to send the proposal to, and make clear they do not want to interfere with creative decisions.

It must be said clearly: **the fan's identity is unknown** and no one has been able to verify that the funds really exist. For now it is a public proposal, not a negotiation.

## Why Ubisoft cancelled The Sands of Time remake

The remake of the 2003 classic was announced in September 2020, with release planned for January 2021. The first trailer was very poorly received because of its graphics, and from there came delays, a change of studio and years of silence.

In January 2026 Ubisoft called time on it, after more than five years of development. The official reason: the game had not reached the expected quality, and finishing it would have required more time and investment than the company could responsibly afford. The decision came during a period of cuts and restructuring for the French publisher.

## Why the offer is causing debate

The **Prince of Persia** series is not going through a happy time. 2024's **The Lost Crown** was praised by critics but did not sell as well as hoped, and its sequel was cancelled. 2025's **The Rogue Prince of Persia** was developed by an external studio, Evil Empire. Fans have therefore welcomed the letter as yet another sign of how much the series is still loved.

Reactions are divided, however. Some call it a lovely gesture, others money down the drain, and some doubt the story is true. On Reddit many point out that 5 million is small compared with the cost of a modern game, and that a cancelled project may already have been written down on the books. Many would rather Ubisoft sold the licence to someone who wants to use it. Closures and cancellations are not limited to Ubisoft: there has also been a lot of talk recently about the [closure of BioWare and the fate of Mass Effect](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/).

## What to expect now

It is very unlikely that Ubisoft will accept funding from an anonymous private individual for a game that has already been cancelled: it would require legal agreements, an available studio and a development plan. More realistically, the letter will remain a symbolic gesture. If Ubisoft comments, we will update this article.
