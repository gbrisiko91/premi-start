**Nioh 3** is coming to Xbox for the first time. **Koei Tecmo** and **Team Ninja** announced it on 24 November in a post on [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/24/nioh-3-xbox-series-debut/): the game will be available on Xbox Series X|S from 28 January 2027, in both the base edition and the Complete Edition.

## Nioh 3 on Xbox: what's in the Complete Edition

The Complete Edition contains the base game and the two expansions, Hell Rising and Bloody Insurrection. According to Xbox Wire, the package brings new eras to explore, additional equipment and skills, plus the endgame modes. Among these is Battlefield of Carnage, a 100-floor challenge designed for those who have already finished the campaign.

On the same day, 28 January, the Complete Edition will also launch on PS5 and Steam: Team Ninja confirmed this on its X profile, presenting the release as part of the celebrations for the Nioh series' tenth anniversary.

## Price and Game Pass: what we don't know yet

At the moment there is no official information on the price in euros, on pre-orders or on a possible arrival on Game Pass. Xbox Wire lists only Xbox Series X|S as the platform, without mentioning the Microsoft Store PC version or Xbox Play Anywhere.

## Another PlayStation exclusive moving to Xbox

Nioh 3 had launched on PS5 and PC, while the series had never been seen on Xbox. The move to Xbox Series X|S adds to the long debate over console exclusives in recent weeks, with Sony meanwhile once again prominently displaying the [PlayStation Exclusive label on Ghost of Yotei and Until Dawn 2](/articoli/esclusive-playstation-marchio/).

For Xbox players it is the first chance to try Team Ninja's soulslike set in feudal Japan, and getting it with both expansions already included isn't bad at all.
