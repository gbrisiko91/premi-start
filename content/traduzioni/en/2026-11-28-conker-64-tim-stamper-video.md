**Tim Stamper**, co-founder of Ultimate Play The Game and **Rare**, has published the first episode of his YouTube channel In Tim's World, and dedicated it to **Twelve Tales: Conker 64**. It is the unreleased Nintendo 64 platformer that Conker's Bad Fur Day grew out of. The video went online on the evening of Thursday 26 November, Italian time.

## What the Conker 64 video shows

Stamper leafs through a folder of drawings and rendered images of the game, almost all dated 1998. According to Nintendo Wire, the video also includes footage of a 1998 build running from a development cartridge: Stamper thinks it was the one taken to an event to show the game.

Anyone who only knows Bad Fur Day will notice the difference straight away. At this stage, Nintendo Wire observes, Conker 64 did not yet have the adult humour that made the squirrel famous.

## From Conker 64 to Conker's Bad Fur Day

The project had been shown at E3 1997 under the name Conker's Quest, and the following year it had become Twelve Tales: Conker 64, with a release planned for autumn 1998. It never reached the shops. Rare turned it into Conker's Bad Fur Day, released on Nintendo 64 in March 2001 in North America and on 6 April 2001 in Europe, with crude humour that made it a cult classic. The squirrel later returned on Xbox with Conker: Live & Reloaded, now among the [original Xbox games playable on PC](/articoli/giochi-xbox-originali-pc/).

## What to expect from In Tim's World

Stamper had reopened the channel on 20 November with a short teaser. In the description he says he has kept documents and artefacts since 1979, and according to Nintendo Wire he wants to open more old folders and talk about other Rare projects from the same period.
