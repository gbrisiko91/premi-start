**GTA 6 is available in Italy according to the official launch time.** At midnight between Wednesday 18 and Thursday 19 November 2026, the scheduled unlock of **Grand Theft Auto VI** kicked in on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is confirmed by the [schedule published by Rockstar support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s), updated on 3 November.

## GTA 6 available in Italy: why it unlocks at midnight

Rockstar chose local midnight on 19 November for both consoles. For players in Italy that means 00:00 CET on Thursday, with no wait for a single worldwide time. The launch therefore rolls out from one country to the next, following the time zones.

New Zealand reached midnight before Europe, while in Italy the scheduled time arrived during the night of 18 November. The official time indicates when the game should become accessible: on its own, it does not guarantee that every single download, account or online service is working without problems.

## GTA 6 preload: what changes from 19 November

Digital preloading had begun on 10 November. On PS5 it opened at local midnight; on Xbox Series X|S at 00:00 GMT, which is 01:00 in Italy. Anyone who has already downloaded the game can check in their library whether the launch lock has been removed.

If the game still appears locked, it is worth checking your connection, the account used for the purchase and any console updates. We have not directly verified how the servers are performing or what access has been like for Italian players, so we are not reporting a technical assessment of the first minute.

## GTA 6 on PC: is there a date?

The 19 November launch covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a release date for PC.
