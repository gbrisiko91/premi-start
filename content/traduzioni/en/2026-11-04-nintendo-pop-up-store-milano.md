The **Nintendo Pop-Up Store** is coming to **Milan**. From **20 November to 20 December 2026**, the home of Mario is opening a temporary shop at **Piazza del Liberty 8**, a stone's throw from the Duomo, with official merchandise and some items usually found only in Japan's Nintendo Stores. For the first three days, entry is by reservation only, with bookings opening on 9 November.

## When the Nintendo Pop-Up Store in Milan opens and where it is

The shop will stay open for exactly one month, **every day from 10:00 to 20:00**, weekends included. The space is large: over **350 square metres** spread across three floors, right in the city centre.

It is Nintendo's second pop-up store in Italy and the first in the heart of Milan. Getting there by public transport is easy:

- **metro**: San Babila (M1 and M4) or Duomo (M1 and M3);
- **tram**: Duomo stop (lines 15 and 16), Piazza Fontana (24) or Teatro alla Scala (1);
- **bus**: Duomo (61) and Via Visconti di Modrone (84).

The timing is no accident: the shop covers the whole Christmas shopping rush, Black Friday included.

## How to book entry to the Nintendo Pop-Up Store

On the first weekend, **from 20 to 22 November**, entry is reserved for those who have booked. Passes can be requested **from 9 November** on the **Nintendo Experience** website, and a **Nintendo Account** is required. Here are the main rules:

- children up to 6 years old do not need to book;
- in groups of two or more people, including young people aged 7 to 17, each person must have a Nintendo Account linked to the same family group;
- **from 23 November** entry is free, without booking, until maximum capacity is reached.

Given the enthusiasm that greets every opening of this kind abroad, it pays to move fast: the first-weekend slots are the ones that will sell out first.

## What you'll find in the Nintendo Pop-Up Store

The shelves will be full of official products dedicated to **Super Mario**, **The Legend of Zelda**, **Animal Crossing**, **Pikmin**, **Splatoon**, **Kirby** and **Metroid**: plushies, pins, keyrings, clothing, bags and homeware, as well as the **My Mario** line. The real attraction, though, is the exclusive items, normally only on sale in Nintendo's shops in Tokyo, Osaka and Kyoto.

It is a golden period for Nintendo fans: between the upcoming Ocarina of Time remake and the big games heading to **Nintendo Switch 2**, such as FromSoftware's eagerly awaited [The Duskbloods, delayed to 2027](/articoli/the-duskbloods-rinvio-2027/), interest in the brand is sky-high.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the Nintendo Pop-Up Store open in Milan?

From 20 November to 20 December 2026, every day from 10:00 to 20:00, at Piazza del Liberty 8.

### Do I need to book to get in?

Only from 20 to 22 November: bookings open on 9 November on Nintendo Experience with a Nintendo Account. From 23 November entry is free, subject to capacity.

### Can you buy consoles and games at the pop-up store?

The shop is mainly dedicated to official merchandise. Some outlets also mention consoles and games: it is worth checking the official website before your visit.
