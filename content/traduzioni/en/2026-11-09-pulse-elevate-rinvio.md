The **Pulse Elevate delay** is official: **Sony** has halted its wireless speakers for **PS5** and PC less than a week before their release, scheduled for 12 November. In a short update on the **PlayStation Blog**, the company explained that quality checks had revealed a production issue. There is no new date.

## Why Sony delayed the Pulse Elevate

The official explanation is terse. Sony speaks of a defect that emerged during quality testing and says it wants to use the extra time to ensure the experience it had promised players. It has not been made clear what the problem is or how many units are affected, and the company apologised to those who were waiting for the product.

The timing surprised everyone: several outlets and creators had already received review units, and some published their first impressions just days before the halt. According to Multiplayer.it, the official PlayStation page now shows a provisional date of 1 January 2027, which looks more like a placeholder than a promise.

## Pulse Elevate price and features in Italy

In Italy the speakers were priced at **€219.99**, and some online shops still appeared ready to deliver them on 12 November even after the announcement. Anyone who pre-ordered them would do well to check the status of their order.

Unveiled in September 2025, the Pulse Elevate are Sony's first desktop speakers designed for gaming. Here is what they promise:

- planar magnetic drivers with built-in woofers;
- support for PS5's **Tempest 3D AudioTech** for more precise positional audio;
- low-latency, lossless **PlayStation Link** connection with PS5, PC, Mac and **PlayStation Portal**, plus simultaneous Bluetooth;
- a microphone in the right speaker with AI-powered noise reduction;
- a rechargeable battery so they can be used away from the desk too.

## The second PlayStation accessory halted in 2026

It is not the first time this year. At the end of July Sony had also indefinitely delayed **FlexStrike**, the arcade controller for fighting games, citing unexpected production delays: there has been no news since. Two accessories halted within a few months have fans debating PlayStation's handling of hardware, just as the competition pushes its own peripherals, such as the [Xbox Nano controller we told you about](/articoli/xbox-nano-controller-uscita/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When are the Pulse Elevate coming out?

There is no new date: Sony has delayed them indefinitely. The PlayStation website shows a provisional date in January 2027, which is not confirmed.

### How much do the Pulse Elevate cost in Italy?

The price listed in Italy is €219.99, around $220 in the United States.

### Do the Pulse Elevate work with PC?

Yes, they connect to PC and Mac via the included PlayStation Link USB-C adapter, as well as to PS5 and PlayStation Portal.
