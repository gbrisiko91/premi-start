**Phantom Blade Zero** is the launch of the autumn. Chinese studio **S-GAME** announced on 2 November that its action game has passed **4 million copies sold in 72 hours** across **PS5** and PC. On Steam the game hit a peak of more than 833,000 concurrent players over its first weekend.

## Phantom Blade Zero: the sales figures

The announcement came from the game's official X account at the end of launch weekend. Phantom Blade Zero came out on 29 October in Europe (the evening of the 28th in the United States) on PS5 and PC, with support for PS5 Pro and Steam Deck, priced at $59.99 for the standard edition.

The player numbers are just as impressive. According to SteamDB statistics reported by several outlets, the game passed 700,000 concurrent users a few hours after release and reached more than **833,000** over the weekend. That is the highest peak of the year for a single-player game on Steam, ahead of **Hollow Knight: Silksong** and **Subnautica 2**.

Critics are on its side too: the Metacritic average is 88 on PC and 87 on PS5, while user reviews on Steam are "Very Positive".

## Why Phantom Blade Zero's success is making headlines

The comparison everyone is making is with **Black Myth: Wukong**, the other big Chinese action game, which in 2024 passed 10 million copies in its first three days. Phantom Blade Zero remains far from that figure, but it confirms that audiences worldwide are responding in huge numbers to action games made in China, a phenomenon that two years ago looked like a one-off.

The result also matters because it comes from a team much smaller than the big Western publishers. The series began as *Rainblood* in 2010, became **Phantom Blade** in 2014 and made a name for itself outside China with *Phantom Blade: Executioners* in 2023. Zero was unveiled at the PlayStation Showcase in May 2023: three years of waiting that have turned into one of the strongest launches of 2026.

The figure is even more striking next to the big Western triple-A games: while the industry grapples with closures and cuts, such as [EA's closure of BioWare](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/), an IP that is new to many Western players sells millions of copies in a weekend.

## What to expect from S-GAME now

The studio has not yet announced additional content or versions for other platforms. For now, no Xbox or Switch 2 versions have been announced. The next milestone to watch is how sales hold up in the weeks of **GTA 6**, which will monopolise players' attention throughout November.
