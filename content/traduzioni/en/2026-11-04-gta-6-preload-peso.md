The **GTA 6 preload** began on **10 November 2026**, nine days before the game's **19 November 2026** release. **Rockstar Games** confirmed the preloading schedule for digital pre-orders on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S** in its Newswire. The stated space requirement was **up to 212 GB depending on the device**, not an identical final installation size on every console.

**After release:** [Grand Theft Auto VI is now available](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39akk424a9oo53/grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available). The dates and times below are a record of the **pre-launch download schedule**, not upcoming events. Rockstar also confirmed on [10 November that preloading was already available](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now).

## When GTA 6 preloading began in Italy

The times vary depending on the console:

- **PlayStation 5**: preloading opened at local midnight on 10 November, which in Italy meant 00:00 at the start of 10 November. The schedule followed the player's local time zone.
- **Xbox Series X|S**: preloading began at the same moment worldwide, at 00:00 GMT on 10 November. In Italy, on winter time, that meant **01:00** on 10 November.

The 10 November opening was earlier than the 12 November timing mentioned in some previous pre-order information. The confirmed schedule above is taken from Rockstar's official pre-load announcement.

## GTA 6 and physical copies with a code in the box

Physical editions containing a download code, rather than a disc, became available from **12 November 2026**. Players could redeem the code and begin downloading once they received it, as [Rockstar confirmed when preloading went live](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now).

On **19 November 2026**, **both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S unlocked at midnight local time**. Rockstar had confirmed that schedule in its [official regional release guide](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s).

## How much space did GTA 6 preloading require?

Rockstar said the **preload required up to 212 GB of available space, depending on the device**. That is a preloading allowance, not confirmation that the downloaded file or installed game was exactly 212 GB on every console. Players with a 512 GB **Xbox Series S** in particular needed to check their available storage before downloading.

The amount of free space needed for later updates can change. Consult your console's storage screen for the current installed size instead of treating the original preload allowance as a permanent requirement.

## Why the GTA 6 preload is so eagerly awaited

Grand Theft Auto VI was one of the biggest releases of November 2026. In the pre-load announcement, **Rockstar** encouraged digital pre-orders to make space ahead of launch; those pre-release preparations are now historical.

At launch, GTA 6 was available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; a PC release date had not been announced. For another major game whose release plans changed, see [The Duskbloods delayed to 2027](/en/articles/the-duskbloods-delayed-2027/).
