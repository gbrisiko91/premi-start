There is a new game on the way for the **Nintendo 64**. It is called **Vasalius: Human Error**, it is a first-person action game with role-playing elements and it runs on the original console, on cartridge. Independent studio **The Retro Room Games** opened its Kickstarter campaign on 18 November, and the voice cast includes **Jon St. John**, the long-time voice actor of Duke Nukem.

## Vasalius: Human Error, what it is about

The story is a science-fiction horror. It all begins the morning after something fell from the sky: an alien presence has infiltrated the city and anyone could be a host in disguise. The protagonist tries to escape together with Mara, a childhood friend, by boarding the last train still running.

The structure recalls 90s shooters: Doom-style 3D levels with enemies drawn as sprites, five weapons, stats that grow with experience (Speed, Reflex, Grit and Charm) and multiple endings. According to tbreak, which draws on the studio's press release, the game runs on a standard 4 MB console, without the Expansion Pak, and saves to battery-backed memory directly on the cartridge.

## Jon St. John and the choice not to use generative AI

Jon St. John lends his voice to the game, and his lines run on Nintendo 64 hardware. For anyone who grew up with Duke Nukem 3D, hearing him on a new cartridge is quite something.

The studio also states that it has not used generative AI, and Time Extension notes that the project puts this front and centre. The graphics are pixel art, created by artists named in the credits.

## Where to play it and how to back it

The campaign's main reward is the boxed physical cartridge. tbreak also reports a PC version on CD and a digital ROM for flash carts and emulators among the rewards. Prices, pledge tiers and delivery dates are on the [Vasalius: Human Error Kickstarter page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/elland/vasalius-human-error).
