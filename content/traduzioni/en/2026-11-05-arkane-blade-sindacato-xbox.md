A new chapter in the story of **Arkane Lyon** and **Marvel's Blade**. The **STJV**, the French union representing workers at the **Dishonored** studio, accuses **Xbox** of deliberately slowing down the game's development by imposing a hiring freeze that lasted 19 months. The statement comes the day after the report of a **Ubisoft** offer to buy the studio.

## Arkane and Blade: what the STJV union says

According to the union, development of Blade is going well and the game is reportedly close to completion, with quality on a par with the studio's previous titles. There have been internal delays, the STJV admits, but they are said to be "artificial": the result of decisions made by **Microsoft**.

The most serious accusation concerns the hiring freeze. For more than a year and a half Arkane Lyon was reportedly unable to bring in new developers, piling up delays and costs that, according to the union, are now being used to justify a possible closure. The STJV speaks openly of a company ready to sabotage the game it funded.

## Why Xbox wants to close or sell Arkane Lyon

The backdrop is the studio "reset" ordered by Xbox CEO **Asha Sharma**, which has led to cuts and sales. For the union, the decision is driven solely by the pursuit of maximum profit: Arkane is said to be profitable, but not enough for Microsoft. The STJV also argues that closing the studio would cost more than finishing the game.

The statement contains two specific demands: giving employees a say in the choice of any buyer and increasing severance pay in the event of closure, which it considers too low. At the time of writing, Xbox has not responded to the accusations.

## What happens now to Marvel's Blade

The studio's fate remains tied to the search for a buyer. As we reported yesterday, [Ubisoft reportedly made an offer for Arkane Lyon](/articoli/arkane-lyon-ubisoft/) along with at least two other companies, according to Bloomberg. Several dates have been circulating: 13 November has been mentioned, while Microsoft has reportedly granted more time until December.

It's worth remembering that the statement is a union position taken in the middle of a negotiation: the accusations have not been independently verified. The fact remains that the fate of one of the most beloved studios in immersive games, also the maker of **Prey** and **Deathloop**, will be decided in the coming weeks. A case that closely recalls that of [EA's possible closure of BioWare](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Marvel's Blade been cancelled?

No. The game is still in development and, according to the union, close to completion. Its future depends on the sale or closure of Arkane Lyon.

### Who is the STJV union?

It's the Syndicat des travailleureuses du jeu vidéo, the French video game workers' union, which also represents Arkane Lyon's employees.

### When will Arkane Lyon's future be decided?

There's no official date. Reports point to mid-November, with a possible slip to December to find a buyer.
