Tomorrow at 8pm the **Worlds 2026** final of **League of Legends** takes place at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn: **Gen.G** against **Bilibili Gaming**. Gen.G have lost just one game in the entire tournament. BLG knocked out T1 in the semi-finals, ending Faker's dream of winning a fourth consecutive world title. Neither of the two mid laners, Chovy and Knight, has ever won a world title.

## How BLG knocked out T1

On Sunday in Allen, Texas, BLG beat T1 3-1. Knight decided game 1 with an Orianna on 8 kills and no deaths. In game 2 T1 levelled with Faker on Syndra, 4/0/13. The last two games both lasted more than 37 minutes and BLG won them, with Viper on Ezreal and Bin on Renekton among the standouts.

According to Sports Kyunghyang, in 13 years no Chinese team had beaten T1 in a best-of-five series in the knockout stage of Worlds. T1 had won the title in 2023, 2024 and 2025 and had played in the last four finals. This year they are out and finish 2026 without silverware.

Two years ago, in London, BLG had lost the world final to T1 themselves, 3-2.

## Gen.G vs BLG: the storylines of the final

### Chovy and Knight chase a first world title

Chovy reaches the final for the first time, after seven Worlds that ended in elimination. Knight had already got there in 2024 with BLG and lost to T1. Hotspawn has dubbed it the final of the "two princes".

### Gen.G have lost just one game

In the quarter-finals they beat LYON 3-0 and in the semi-finals Invictus Gaming 3-0. In the whole tournament they have never had to play a game 4: according to Hotspawn, BLG have not yet seen everything Gen.G can do.

### BLG have lost their last two finals

They lost the Worlds 2024 final to T1 and the July MSI final to Hanwha Life, both 3-2. In 2023, however, it was BLG who knocked out Gen.G and Chovy in the quarter-finals, also 3-2.

### A Korean champion in the Chinese team

BLG's marksman is the Korean Viper, who won Worlds in 2021 with EDward Gaming: since then no Chinese team has won the title. Gen.G have two former world champions, Ruler (2017) and Canyon (2020).

## Start time and where to watch the Worlds 2026 final

The final is on **Saturday 14 November at 8pm Italian time** at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York, the first world final on the US East Coast. It is a best-of-five with Fearless Draft, so each champion can only be used once in the series.

It is streamed free on the [LoL Esports Twitch and YouTube channels](https://lolesports.com/). Before the match there is the opening ceremony with Coco Jones and Vic Mensa, Sofía Reyes and Jeon Soyeon, and the tournament anthem, "Know My Name" by True Damage. The winning team takes home $1 million.
