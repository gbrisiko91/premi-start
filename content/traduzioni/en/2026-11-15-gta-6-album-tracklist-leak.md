Four days before the release of **Grand Theft Auto VI**, an alleged tracklist for **GTA VI: The Album** is doing the rounds online. Images of the back of a vinyl record, shared on 14 November by GTA-focused accounts and by sites such as RockstarINTEL and Dexerto, list **34 songs** and artists including **Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey and Jennie**. It is a leak: **Rockstar Games has not confirmed the full tracklist or all the names reported**.

## Where the alleged 34-song list comes from

According to [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/full-gta-vi-the-album-track-list-leaked/), the material was reportedly shared by a record shop worker who filmed the back of a vinyl copy. The list is split across four sides. The footage has been picked up online, but the origin of the record and the authenticity of the whole pressing **have not been confirmed by Rockstar or Atlantic Records**.

Some titles on the list match songs Rockstar has already made public, including *That's It* by Yung Lean and *RHYNO* by Travis Scott. That overlap makes the document plausible, but it **does not prove that all the other names are correct**.

## Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey and the other names in the leak

Artists appearing on the alleged list include:

- **Dua Lipa** with *Go Easy* and **Lana Del Rey** with *Crazy for You*;
- **Jennie** with *Power Trip*, plus **Camila Cabello**, **Zara Larsson** and **Rina Sawayama**;
- **Playboi Carti**, **Cardi B**, **Gunna**, **Young Thug** and **Burna Boy**;
- **Justice**, who would also appear with Michael McDonald and in a collaboration with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard;
- **Keith Richards** and a track credited to Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes and Robert Glasper.

These are **credits that appear on the leaked tracklist**, not official confirmation that each artist is involved. The various transcriptions circulating online also show small differences in the titles, another reason to wait for the final list.

## What Rockstar has actually announced

In its [17 September announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19), Rockstar confirmed that *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* contains **34 original songs**, produced in collaboration with Atlantic Records. At the time it unveiled six singles, including tracks by Travis Scott, Yung Lean, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro and Keith Richards.

The date **announced by Rockstar is 19 November 2026**, alongside the game. For the physical versions, however, some retailer listings give **20 November**: for example, the vinyl listing on [Record Store Day](https://recordstoreday.com/UPC/075678576454). This difference in retail availability does not, on its own, prove an official delay to the digital release.

The album is separate from the in-game radio stations and from the instrumental score. The latter, **Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score**, is composed by **Justice**: [Rockstar has confirmed directly](https://www.rockstargames.com/it/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice) **30 tracks and a release on 11 December 2026**.

## When will we know the final tracklist?

The full list can be considered official once it is published by Rockstar, Atlantic Records or music services through verifiable listings. Until then, the names that emerged from the vinyl should be treated as **unconfirmed**, even when several outlets report them.
