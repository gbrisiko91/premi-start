**Arkane Lyon** could get a new French owner. According to a report from **Bloomberg**, **Ubisoft** has made an offer to **Xbox** to acquire the **Dishonored** studio, which Microsoft is trying to sell. It is not alone: at least two other companies in the industry are said to have submitted proposals. None of the parties has confirmed anything yet.

## Arkane Lyon and Ubisoft: what the Bloomberg report says

The news comes from **Jason Schreier**, one of Bloomberg's most reliable journalists when it comes to the industry. Ubisoft is the only buyer named, while the other two bidders remain anonymous. The terms of any deal have not been set.

According to the same report, Microsoft is also considering a different route: selling the studio to its own management and making it independent. That is what has already happened with **Double Fine** and **Compulsion Games** in recent months.

An Xbox spokesperson told Windows Central that the company is actively exploring options ahead of a deadline set for December, without commenting on individual negotiations.

## Why Xbox wants to sell Arkane Lyon

It all stems from the studio "reset" ordered by Xbox's new CEO, **Asha Sharma**, to cut costs: layoffs, mergers and the decision, announced in July, to part ways with five internal studios. Arkane Lyon entered a consultation period required under French labour law.

In recent weeks a mid-November deadline had emerged: without a buyer, the studio risked closure, with around **217 jobs** at risk. It was later reported that Xbox had granted more time, until mid-December.

## What happens to Marvel's Blade

The project at stake is **Marvel's Blade**, the action game about the vampire hunter announced at **The Game Awards 2023**. According to people at the studio who spoke to Kotaku, the game is close to completion: a sale would allow Arkane Lyon to finish it, while a closure would almost certainly cancel it.

The fate of the studio's series, **Dishonored** and **Deathloop**, is also unclear: it is not known whether the rights would follow the team or stay with Microsoft.

## Why Ubisoft would make sense

Ubisoft is headquartered in France and already has a large network of studios in the country, so an acquisition in Lyon would be a natural fit logistically. It would not even be the first time the two companies have done business: in 2023 Microsoft sold Ubisoft the cloud streaming rights to Activision Blizzard games for 15 years, to win approval from the UK regulator.

However, this is not the easiest time for Ubisoft, amid layoffs and cancelled projects such as the **Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time** remake. That is why the move has surprised many observers. The case is reminiscent of another historic studio hanging in the balance, as we reported when covering the [possible closure of BioWare by EA](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Ubisoft bought Arkane Lyon?

No, for now it is an offer reported by Bloomberg. Neither Ubisoft nor Microsoft has confirmed a deal.

### Has Marvel's Blade been cancelled?

No. The game is still in development, but its future depends on the studio being sold before the December deadline.

### What games has Arkane Lyon made?

Arkane Lyon's games include Dishonored, Dishonored 2 and Deathloop, and it is working on Marvel's Blade.
