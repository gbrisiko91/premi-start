As of today, 7 November, the Straw Hat crew is complete in **Fortnite**. **Zoro** and **Sanji** arrive in the Item Shop together with **Chopper**, who joins as a Sidekick. It is the second wave of the collaboration with **One Piece**, the most eagerly awaited ever by fans of the anime and one of the biggest **Epic Games** has ever put together.

## All the One Piece skins in Fortnite

The **Treasure Hunters** mini-season kicked off on 1 November and brings the characters in two different ways:

- **Battle Pass:** Ace and Nico Robin.
- **Item Shop from 1 November:** Luffy, Nami and Usopp.
- **Item Shop from today, 7 November:** Zoro, Sanji and Chopper as a Sidekick.

Buggy is also part of the collaboration. Epic did not want a simple skin bundle: the **Going Merry**, the crew's ship, takes the place of the classic Battle Bus, and the map is filled with locations inspired by the world of One Piece.

## The Fortnite x One Piece trailer that looks like an anime opening

For the launch, **Toei Animation**, the studio that produces the anime, made a two-minute trailer paying homage to the series' first opening theme, **"We Are!"** by Hiroshi Kitadani. Luffy and friends run alongside classic Fortnite characters such as Jones, Peely and Fishstick, drawn in the anime's style. The video exists in English and in Japanese, but in both the song remains the original Japanese one.

It is a clear signal: for Epic this is no ordinary collab, but an event to be treated like a special episode.

## The Pirate Token and the treasure hunt

The real masterstroke of the season is still the **Pirate Shop Token**: an extremely rare reward hidden in chests that makes almost the entire Item Shop free for 14 days, One Piece skins included. The community estimates that it drops roughly once in every 700,000, and a black market of accounts and paid "carries" has already sprung up around the Token. We told the whole story in our [article on the Pirate Shop Token](/articoli/fortnite-gettone-negozio-pirata/).

From today the hunt has one more reason: whoever finds the Token can also get the newly arrived Zoro and Sanji for free.

## Why Epic is pushing so hard right now

The timing does not seem accidental. **GTA VI** comes out in a few weeks, and both Kotaku and Everyeye read this season's moves as a way to keep players on Fortnite before the arrival of Rockstar's giant. A collaboration with one of the most popular anime in the world, rolled out in weekly waves, is a great reason to come back every day.

The Treasure Hunters mini-season runs until early December: after One Piece, other guests will arrive, including Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do Zoro and Sanji arrive in Fortnite?

Today, 7 November, in the Item Shop, together with Chopper as a Sidekick.

### Which One Piece characters are in the Battle Pass?

Ace and Nico Robin. Luffy, Nami, Usopp, Zoro and Sanji can be bought in the Item Shop.

### How long does the Fortnite x One Piece collaboration last?

The Treasure Hunters mini-season runs from 1 November to early December.
