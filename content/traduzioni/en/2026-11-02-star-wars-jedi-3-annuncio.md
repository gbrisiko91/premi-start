**Star Wars Jedi 3** is at an advanced stage of development and should be announced soon. That's what Bloomberg's **Jason Schreier** wrote on 30 October, answering readers' questions after his investigation into the closure of **BioWare**. On the same occasion he added that **Star Wars Battlefront 3**, the project that according to Bloomberg **EA** chose over the new Mass Effect, has not yet entered production.

This is not an official announcement: EA and **Respawn Entertainment** have not commented.

## What Jason Schreier said about Star Wars Jedi 3

Asked whether BioWare's closure put the third Jedi game at risk, Schreier replied that the game "is still coming" and should be unveiled soon. Asked whether Jedi 3 could end up competing internally with Battlefront 3, he explained that the former is very far along in development, while the latter has not even started.

That a third instalment exists is not news. In September 2024, during EA's investor day, **Laura Miele** confirmed that Respawn was working on the final chapter of **Cal Kestis**'s story, which began with **Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order** in 2019 and continued with **Star Wars Jedi: Survivor** in 2023. The title, platforms and release date are still missing.

## Why it matters: BioWare closed for a game that doesn't exist yet

Schreier's remark has reignited the controversy over [BioWare's closure and the choice of Battlefront 3](/articoli/bioware-chiusa-battlefront-3/). According to Bloomberg, EA weighed up two very expensive science-fiction projects, Battlefront 3 and the next Mass Effect, and chose Star Wars. Finding out that the game is not even in development yet has made fans of the Shepard saga even angrier.

EA, for its part, reiterated on 30 October that the decision came after months of planning and analysis, without addressing the comparison with Star Wars. The community's anger has also reached the streets: according to Multiplayer.it, EA's offices in Madrid were reportedly daubed with "Save Mass Effect" graffiti.

## When Star Wars Jedi 3 could be announced

Schreier did not mention dates or events. Among fans, **The Game Awards** in December is being floated, a traditional showcase for big announcements, but for now it is only speculation. The return of **Cameron Monaghan** as Cal, taken for granted by many, has not been officially confirmed.

If you want to catch up on the series before the third instalment, Star Wars Jedi: Survivor is available on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, including on the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1774580).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Star Wars Jedi 3 been announced?

Not yet with a trailer or a title. EA confirmed in 2024 that the final chapter is in development at Respawn, and according to Jason Schreier the proper announcement will come soon.

### When does Star Wars Jedi 3 come out?

There is no date. The game does not yet have an official launch window.

### Is Star Wars Battlefront 3 in development?

According to Schreier, no: the project, for which EA reportedly sacrificed the new Mass Effect, has not yet entered production. EA has not officially announced any new Battlefront.
