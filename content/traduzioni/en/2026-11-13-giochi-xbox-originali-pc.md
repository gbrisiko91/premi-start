**Original Xbox games** are landing on PC in force. On 12 November, to celebrate 25 years of Microsoft's first console, **Xbox backward compatibility on PC** went from 4 to 25 titles, with the arrival of 21 classics including **Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic**, The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind and TimeSplitters 2. They are available right now on Windows 11.

It is the most nostalgic news of the day and, for anyone who grew up with the big black-and-green box from 2001, one of the most interesting too.

## The full list of 25 original Xbox games on PC

The four titles already available (BLiNX: The Time Sweeper, Conker: Live and Reloaded, Crimson Skies: High Road to Revenge and Fuzion Frenzy) are joined by these 21, as reported in the [official XBOX Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/25-original-xbox-games-now-available-on-pc/):

- Armed and Dangerous
- Disney's Chicken Little
- The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind
- Grabbed by the Ghoulies
- Indiana Jones and the Emperor's Tomb
- Red Faction II
- Secret Weapons Over Normandy
- Star Wars Battlefront and Star Wars Battlefront II
- Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith
- Star Wars Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy and Jedi Knight II: Jedi Outcast
- Star Wars: Jedi Starfighter and Star Wars: Starfighter Special Edition
- Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic and KOTOR II: The Sith Lords
- Star Wars: Republic Commando
- Star Wars: The Clone Wars
- Thrillville
- TimeSplitters 2 and TimeSplitters: Future Perfect

The number of Star Wars games stands out: no fewer than 13 of the 21 new arrivals. Morrowind, Conker, Grabbed by the Ghoulies, BLiNX, Crimson Skies and Fuzion Frenzy are also included in Game Pass.

## How Xbox backward compatibility on PC works

You need Windows 11 and to download the game. Depending on the case, you either have to buy it or have a Game Pass subscription, and the service is only available in some countries. Microsoft points out that features vary from title to title.

All the games now support mouse and keyboard. The four titles from the first wave have also received performance and stability improvements and run on handhelds such as the ROG Xbox Ally family.

## KOTOR and Conker get achievements after twenty years

The surprise is for score hunters: for the first time KOTOR, KOTOR II, BLiNX and Conker: Live and Reloaded have Xbox achievements, which can also be unlocked on Xbox One and Series X|S after the latest system update. Some may unlock based on progress you have already made, and Microsoft promises to extend them to other games from the first Xbox.

Morrowind has always been on PC, but for many the Xbox version was their first contact with the series, and replaying it now is a nice refresher while we wait for news on [The Elder Scrolls 6 and Project Sentinel](/articoli/elder-scrolls-6-sentinel/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you play original Xbox games on PC?

Through Xbox backward compatibility on PC: you need Windows 11, the game has to be downloaded and, depending on the title, bought or included in Game Pass.

### Does Star Wars KOTOR for Xbox on PC have achievements?

Yes: since 12 November 2026 KOTOR and KOTOR II have Xbox achievements for the first time, on console too.

### How many original Xbox games are available on PC?

Twenty-five: the four already available plus the 21 added on 12 November 2026.
