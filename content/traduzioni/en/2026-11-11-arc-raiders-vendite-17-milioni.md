**ARC Raiders** has passed **17 million copies sold**. **Nexon** announced the milestone on 10 November while presenting its third-quarter 2026 results: just over a year after launch, **Embark Studios**' extraction shooter keeps selling, driven by October's major **Frozen Trail** update. But the publisher is already warning that year-end revenue will be lower than in 2025.

## ARC Raiders sales: Nexon's official figures

In its [official press release](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Nexon-Releases-Earnings-for-Third-Quarter-2026), Nexon writes that the game has passed 17 million units sold since its release on 30 October 2025 on **PC**, **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. In May the counter stood at 16 million: according to estimates reported by VGTimes, around 700,000 copies were added in the last quarter.

According to Nexon, the credit goes to **Frozen Trail**, released on 8 October: the biggest update since launch, which brought back many players who had stopped playing and generated new sales. For the Korean-Japanese publisher, the third quarter closed in line with expectations, with total revenue of ¥138.4 billion, up 17% year on year.

## Why Nexon expects a decline at the end of 2026

The comparison with 2025, however, is harsh. A year ago ARC Raiders had just launched and was one of the best-selling games of the moment. That is why Nexon forecasts fourth-quarter revenue between ¥107.7 billion and ¥118.9 billion, down 4 to 13% year on year, with ARC Raiders contributing less than last year but holding steady compared with the summer.

Propping up the numbers is **MapleStory**, which posted its best quarter ever with revenue up 72%. CEO Junghun Lee stressed that betting on multiple series reduces dependence on a single franchise.

## The future of ARC Raiders

Seventeen million remains a huge result for a new game not tied to a famous series. The challenge now is keeping the community alive in its second year, with regular updates like Frozen Trail. Film and TV adaptations are also in the works, while according to GamesRadar+ Embark has already started development on two new projects.
