**Fairgames** has been cancelled. **PlayStation** has halted development of the multiplayer shooter from **Haven Studios**, the Montréal studio founded by Jade Raymond: the news was first reported by Bloomberg on 10 November and then confirmed by Sony. The reason is a player test that failed to deliver the hoped-for results.

## Why Fairgames was cancelled

According to the internal email sent to staff by executives **Sean Decker** and **John Doyle**, reported by Bloomberg and several other outlets, the latest public test went well from a technical and server-management standpoint. The problem was the players: in short, their feedback and the time they spent in matches fell short of the targets that had been set. A competitive shooter market described as tough also weighed on the decision.

In its official statement, PlayStation speaks of a "difficult decision" and points out that no staff cuts are planned at the moment. It is a significant call, because the game had been in development for around three years and was the studio's only announced project.

## What happens now to Haven Studios

Haven is not closing. The studio will collaborate with other **PlayStation Studios** teams and explore new ideas, but Sony has not said which games it will work on. In practice, five years after its founding, Haven goes from being a studio with its own flagship project to a support team.

Fairgames was unveiled in May 2023 on the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=379942) as a competitive heist experience for **PS5** and **PC**: squads of players chasing the money of the rich, in matches that mixed player-versus-player combat with computer-controlled enemies. After that trailer the game vanished from the radar, amid changes of direction, the departure of founder Jade Raymond in 2025 and cancellation rumours that had already been denied last year.

## Yet another setback for Sony's live-service games

The cancellation adds to a long list. In recent years Sony has halted **The Last of Us** multiplayer, shut down **Concord** after just a few days and abandoned several never-announced projects, scaling back a plan that called for more than ten live-service games. At the same time, the company keeps betting on big single-player games, as we explained when discussing the [future of PlayStation exclusives](/articoli/esclusive-playstation-marchio/).

The good news, at least for those working at Haven, is that there are no layoffs. The less good news is that PlayStation's online games strategy keeps losing pieces.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will Fairgames ever come out?

No: PlayStation has stopped development of the game, which never had a release date or pre-orders.

### Is Haven Studios closing?

No, the studio remains open and PlayStation says there will be no layoffs; it will work with other PlayStation Studios teams.

### What kind of game was Fairgames?

A competitive heist-themed online shooter for PS5 and PC, announced by Haven Studios in May 2023.
