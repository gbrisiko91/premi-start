The **Commodore 64 Ultimate** is about to return to shop shelves, the first time in more than 30 years for a **Commodore**-branded computer. This week the company announced an exclusive distribution deal with **Colby**, which will bring the C64 reissue to physical stores in Europe and parts of Africa and Asia. The retailers mentioned include **MediaMarkt** and **Argos**.

## Where you will be able to buy the Commodore 64 Ultimate

Until now the C64 Ultimate could only be ordered from Commodore's official website. With the deal announced on X, the machine joins Colby's network: according to Everyeye, the distributor has around 70 retail partners and more than 500 points of sale, and already works with publishers such as **SEGA**, **Konami** and **Bandai**.

Commodore named MediaMarkt and Argos as examples, promising more stores soon, and said it is also working on the United States. The arrival in shops is expected over the Christmas period, but no exact date has been announced yet.

## Is the Commodore 64 Ultimate coming to Italy?

For now Italy is not explicitly mentioned. The country falls within the European area covered by the deal and MediaMarkt has many stores there, but there is no list of Italian retailers or a price in euros yet. As Q-Gin points out, the mention of the chains does not mean the computer will be available in all of their stores.

On the official website prices are in dollars. For the new **64C Ultimate** line, with a slim case inspired by the 1986 model, they range from $299.99 for the BASIC Beige version to $499.99 for the Founder's Edition, with the Starlight Edition, featuring a transparent shell and LED lights, at $349.99.

## What the Commodore 64 Ultimate is

It is not a mini console with preinstalled games, but a genuine full-size reissue of the best-selling computer in history. Inside is an **FPGA** chip that reproduces the original hardware rather than emulating it in software, with claimed compatibility of over 99.9%.

The keyboard is full-size, and the vintage ports for cartridges, cassette recorders and floppy drives are joined by **HDMI** output and USB ports. The box includes a cassette-shaped USB stick with more than a hundred games, demos and programs. For the new 64C, Commodore even tracked down the original 1986 moulds.

It is yet another sign of how alive the retro hardware market is: just think of [Hyperkin's Mega95, the handheld Mega Drive](/articoli/hyperkin-mega95-mega-drive-portatile/) that fans have been waiting on for years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the Commodore 64 Ultimate coming to shops?

Commodore says it is coming "soon" and the period indicated is Christmas, but there is no official date yet.

### How much does the Commodore 64 Ultimate cost?

On the official website the 64C Ultimate line starts at $299.99. Euro prices for shops have not been announced yet.

### Is the C64 Ultimate an emulator?

No: it uses an FPGA chip that recreates the original Commodore 64 hardware and is compatible with vintage cartridges, cassettes and floppy disks.
