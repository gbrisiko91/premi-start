The **The Game Awards 2026** nominations have a date: **Geoff Keighley** will read them out in a live stream on Tuesday 17 November at 18:00 Italian time. The ceremony will take place on 10 December at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. And there is already a talking point: **GTA 6** comes out two days after the nominees are announced, yet it can still be nominated.

## When The Game Awards 2026 nominations are announced

The organisers confirmed the date in a post on X on 13 November. The nominations show starts at 17:00 in the UK, which is 18:00 in Italy (midday on the US East Coast).

Until then, the official website only said "mid-November". Just over three weeks will pass between the nominee announcement and the awards night, and in Italy the live broadcast from Los Angeles on 10 December will fall during the night.

## GTA 6 at The Game Awards 2026: why it can be nominated

The rules say a game is eligible if it is on sale by Friday 20 November 2026 at 21:00 Pacific Time (6am on Saturday 21 in Italy). Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto 6 comes out on 19 November, so it makes it by one day.

For the public vote, the Players' Voice, the deadline is even more generous: a game just has to be out by 30 November. On paper, then, GTA 6 can compete in every category, Game of the Year included.

The same applies to the Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake on Switch 2, released on 5 November and therefore eligible. If you missed the press scores, here are [the Ocarina of Time reviews on Switch 2](/articoli/zelda-ocarina-of-time-recensioni/).

## Why GTA 6's eligibility is causing debate

The nominations are announced on the 17th, the game hits shops on the 19th. The voters, that is the jury of outlets and journalists chosen by the organisers, may have played it little or not at all.

According to Men's Journal, if GTA 6 ended up among the nominees it would be the first time a game had been nominated before being available to the public. Former juror Alanah Pearce said voting "usually" starts "a month before", but that is a personal account: there is no public information on when voting began this year or on when reviewers received the game.

On social media, some take a flood of nominations for granted while others think jurors cannot judge a game they have never seen. Others fear that, for exactly this reason, Rockstar will be left out of the main category. We will be watching the live stream on Tuesday at 18:00.
