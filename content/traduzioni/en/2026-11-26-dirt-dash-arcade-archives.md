Today, 26 November, **Dirt Dash** joins **Hamster**’s **Arcade Archives** catalogue. It is the off-road racing game that Namco brought to arcades in 1995, the same year as Sega Rally Championship. It launches simultaneously on Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PS4, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Dirt Dash on Arcade Archives: versions and prices

There are two versions, as is now often the case with Hamster’s releases.

- Arcade Archives Dirt Dash, for Switch and PS4, at $14.99
- Arcade Archives 2 Dirt Dash, for Switch 2, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, at $16.99

Anyone who buys the Switch or PS4 version first can upgrade to the Switch 2 or PS5 version for $2.99, on the same account. Save data, however, does not carry over from one version to the other.

## The modes: from high scores to Caravan

Alongside the original game there are the Hi Score and Caravan modes, both with online leaderboards, plus the series’ usual options: difficulty, customisable controls, multiple save slots, rewind and filters that mimic a CRT screen. The Arcade Archives 2 version adds an exclusive Time Attack mode and VRR support. The original’s Network mode, on the other hand, is missing.

## The Namco rally game that answered Sega Rally

Dirt Dash ran on Namco’s Super System 22 board and reached arcades the same year as Sega Rally Championship, so much so that Time Extension describes it as Namco’s answer to Sega’s rally game. For those who only remember it from the cabinet, it is a welcome and unexpected revival.
