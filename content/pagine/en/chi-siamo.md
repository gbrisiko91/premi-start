## Why "Soffia Cartuccia"

*Soffia cartuccia* is Italian for "blow the cartridge". If you owned a NES, a Super Nintendo or a Mega Drive, you know the move: the game won't start, so you pull out the cartridge, blow into it with conviction and slot it back in. Nobody ever knew whether it really worked, but for a whole generation of players it was the ritual that stood between a black screen and the game.

We chose the name because we like looking ahead without forgetting where we come from. We cover the most anticipated releases and the big industry stories, but whenever there's a great **retro gaming** story (a prototype rediscovered, a secret found after thirty years, a vintage console brought back to life) we put it front and centre.

## The Hype bar

Next to every article you'll find a bar with a score from 0 to 100: our **Hype index**. It measures how much a story is being talked about, not how good or important it is. We work it out by looking at:

- how many different outlets are picking it up;
- how much buzz it has on social media, Reddit and YouTube;
- how much it matters to players in Italy, where the site is based;
- how fresh it is;
- how surprising or divisive it is.

The **Hype chart** on the front page lines up the hottest stories of the week.
