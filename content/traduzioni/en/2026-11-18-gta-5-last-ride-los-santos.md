On Wednesday **18 November 2026**, the eve of **GTA 6**'s release, **GTA Online** players are getting together for **The Last Ride**: a drive to bid a symbolic farewell to Los Santos, the virtual city of **GTA 5** that has kept them company for thirteen years.

The idea was popularised on TikTok by creator **CraftizyTV**. It is not an official Rockstar Games event and there is no single session bringing everyone together: groups of players are organising their own meet-ups.

## The Last Ride: meeting point and route

The route proposed by the community starts at the entrance to **Los Santos International Airport**. From there participants cross the city via **Del Perro Pier**, head up the coast towards **Paleto Bay** and finish the journey on **Mount Chiliad**.

It is a suggested itinerary, not a mandatory course set by Rockstar: each group can adapt it. The aim is to take the time to cross the map together, rather than turning the meet-up into yet another lobby battle.

The suggested rules are simple:

- no weapons or shooting during the drive;
- avoid ramming and police chases;
- respect the other participants and stay in the convoy;
- bring your favourite vehicle, with no required model.

## What time it starts and how to take part

**No single start time has been announced for all platforms and all time zones.** 18 November is the day chosen for the farewell, but each community can set a different departure time.

To take part, it is best to arrange things with a group of friends or a community organising a convoy in **GTA Online**, checking the platform, game version and instructions for your meet-up. Anyone using an **invite-only session** will need to receive the group's invitation: jumping into a random public lobby does not guarantee you will find The Last Ride.

**A verifiable example for PS5:** organiser **MattyRyoPlays** posted [an invitation to his convoy on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/gta5/comments/1xw1y5y/gta_online_the_last_ride_around_los_santos_nov/) for **Wednesday 18 November at 21:00 GMT, which is 22:00 in Italy (CET)**. In his announcement he asks people to go through the Discord group to get an invitation to the PlayStation 5 session. **That time applies only to his meet-up**, not to every community.

Each standard GTA Online session hosts up to **30 players**, so the meet-ups take place in separate groups rather than one big worldwide lobby. Gfinity Esports points, for example, to a gathering for **PS5** users coordinated by creator **All Eyez On Glitch**.

There does not appear to be a specific entry ticket for The Last Ride, but the usual requirements for accessing GTA Online on your platform apply. There is no official sign-up form run by Rockstar.

## A symbolic farewell, not the end of GTA Online

**The Last Ride does not mean GTA Online is being switched off on 19 November.** No shutdown of its servers has been announced to coincide with the arrival of GTA 6. It is a way of remembering a game released in September 2013 that grew up with its community.

"For a lot of us it wasn't just a game: it was years of memories," CraftizyTV told GamesRadar+. YouTuber Dezoh also shared a personal drive around Los Santos, recalling that he was thirteen or fourteen when GTA 5 came out and is now twenty-seven.
