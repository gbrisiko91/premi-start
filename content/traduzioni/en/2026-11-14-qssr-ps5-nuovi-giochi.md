**PS5**'s **QSSR** is expanding. On Friday 13 November, **Sony** announced on the PlayStation Blog six new games compatible with its artificial intelligence-based upscaling: Marvel's Spider-Man 2, Control Resonant, ARC Raiders, The Finals, No Man's Sky and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam. Spider-Man 2 has already received the update; the others will follow in the coming weeks.

## What is PS5's QSSR

QSSR stands for Quick Spectral Super Resolution. It is the AI upscaling technology Sony unveiled in October for the PS5 we already have under the TV, with no need for the Pro model. It is built on the same foundation as PS5 Pro's PSSR and shares its interface, so integration is simpler for developers who already support PSSR.

Sony explains that QSSR comes from Project Amethyst, its ongoing collaboration with AMD. The first games to use it were Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei, where it appears as an additional graphics option.

## PS5 games with QSSR and when they arrive

Here is what each studio has announced:

- Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac Games): available now, in Fidelity and Performance modes, with a more stable and sharper image.
- Control Resonant (Remedy Entertainment): with an update in December, for greater image stability and more fine detail.
- ARC Raiders (Embark Studios): "coming soon", with no date, with more sharpness and clearer environments.
- The Finals (Embark Studios): also "soon", with the same benefits as ARC Raiders.
- No Man's Sky (Hello Games): expected in the coming weeks, with cleaner detail while aiming for the best possible frame rate.
- Hell Let Loose: Vietnam (Expression Games): with a future update, to improve visuals in the jungle and in large-scale battles.

Sony adds that more games will follow in the coming months.

## Why QSSR matters to base PS5 owners

The biggest name is ARC Raiders, which keeps racking up players: we covered it when reporting on [ARC Raiders sales passing 17 million](/articoli/arc-raiders-vendite-17-milioni/). For those playing it on a standard PS5, a cleaner image without changing console is a welcome gift.

And there are a lot of base PS5s. Sony has just passed 100 million consoles sold, as we wrote when covering the [100 million PS5 milestone](/articoli/ps5-100-milioni-console-vendute/), and the Pro is only a part of that figure. Digital Foundry, which has tested QSSR, described its potential as "remarkable".

Sony has not yet said how close QSSR will get to the quality of PSSR on Pro, nor whether it will also come to older games in the catalogue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does QSSR work on the standard PS5?

Yes. Sony presented it as AI upscaling for the PS5 you already own, with no need for a PS5 Pro.

### Which games support QSSR on PS5?

Currently Marvel's Wolverine, Ghost of Yōtei and Marvel's Spider-Man 2. Control Resonant arrives in December; ARC Raiders, The Finals, No Man's Sky and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam in the coming weeks.

### What is the difference between QSSR and PSSR?

PSSR is PS5 Pro's upscaling. QSSR is built on the same foundation and is designed for the standard PS5.
