**God of War Sons of Sparta** is coming to **PS4**. The 2D platformer from **Santa Monica Studio** and Mega Cat Studios, which shadow-dropped on PS5 in February, will be available on Sony's older console from 16 November. On the same day, a free update adds two new modes on both PS4 and PS5.

## When God of War Sons of Sparta comes out on PS4

The date is Monday 16 November, starting at midnight local time. Sony announced it on 13 November together with the two studios, and the news was picked up by VGC and Multiplayer.it.

There is no price for the PS4 version yet. On PS5 the game costs €29.99 for the standard edition and €39.99 for the Digital Deluxe. Nor has it been said whether those who buy on PS4 will also get the PS5 version.

## Hermes and Erebus: the new free modes

Hermes mode is designed for speedrunning: it adds a timer with splits and a leaderboard that stays saved. It unlocks once you finish the game, or with a code on the title menu that will make anyone who grew up with Konami games smile: up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, L1, R1, touchpad.

Erebus mode is the one for masochists: Kratos and enemies die from a single hit. Both are selected when starting a new game and stay tied to that save slot: according to Multiplayer.it, once chosen they cannot be changed.

## What is God of War Sons of Sparta

It is a 2D action platformer with pixel art environments, inspired by the classics. It tells the story of Kratos's youth during his training at the Agoge alongside his brother Deimos, in the region of Laconia, and is considered part of the series canon. It was written by the same narrative team behind God of War (2018), Ragnarök and Valhalla.

Kratos fights with a customisable spear and shield, three skill trees and the Gifts of Olympus, divine artefacts that unlock special attacks. The music is once again by Bear McCreary, and TC Carson returns as the narrating voice of adult Kratos. The game came out in February during the same State of Play in which Sony confirmed the remake of the Greek trilogy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is God of War Sons of Sparta available on PS4?

It will be from 16 November 2026. Until now the game had only been released on PS5, in February 2026.

### How do you unlock Hermes mode in God of War Sons of Sparta?

By finishing the game once, or by entering the sequence up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, L1, R1, touchpad on the title menu.

### How much does God of War Sons of Sparta cost?

On PS5 the standard edition costs €29.99 and the Digital Deluxe €39.99. The price of the PS4 version has not been announced yet.
