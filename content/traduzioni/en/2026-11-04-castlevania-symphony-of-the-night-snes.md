**Castlevania: Symphony of the Night** runs on the **Super Nintendo**. It is not an official **Konami** release but the work of a fan: independent developer **Kaffeeware** has published a free demo of their fan port, which brings the 1997 PlayStation masterpiece to a console that never had it. And they stress that they made it without using artificial intelligence.

## Castlevania Symphony of the Night on SNES: what's in the demo

The demo was released as a Halloween gift and can be downloaded for free from [Kaffeeware's itch.io page](https://kaffeeware.itch.io/sotn-snes). It covers roughly the **first half of the game**: you explore Dracula's castle as **Alucard** up to the famous mid-game twist, which is not included here.

The port works in emulators but also on real hardware: modded SNES consoles or ones with a flash cartridge, and re-releases such as the **Analogue Super Nt**. The author asks players to report bugs and give feedback on itch.io and in the comments on YouTube, where they have been posting updates on the project since June 2025.

## How to get Symphony of the Night running on the Super Nintendo

Kaffeeware's stated goal is to stay faithful to the **original SNES specifications**. Achieving that requires compromises: the ROM is larger than those of the era to fit all the graphics, and some rooms have fewer zombies on screen, which also behave slightly differently.

The work was done "by eye", by watching the original game rather than directly converting its code: so it is not a perfect copy, but a reconstruction. According to Multiplayer.it, the developer is considering using the decompilation projects of the PlayStation game to make the conversion more accurate.

## The inverted castle won't be coming

Anyone hoping for a complete version should make peace with it. Asked whether they would bring the whole game to SNES, Kaffeeware gave a firm answer: no, and certainly not the inverted castle, the second half that made Symphony of the Night legendary.

It is not the first feat of this kind: back in 2024 another fan project showed a conversion of the game for the **Mega Drive**, Sega's historic rival.

## Why everyone is talking about it

Symphony of the Night is one of the games that defined the **metroidvania** genre, and seeing it run on a 16-bit console spread across retro gaming forums within days. The author's claim of not having used generative AI added fuel to the discussion, at a time when platforms like itch.io are clearing out AI-made games.

The usual legal question remains: this is an unauthorised project based on a Konami property, and the company has not always tolerated fan games in the past. For now the demo is available. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Was Castlevania: Symphony of the Night ever released on SNES?

No. The game came out in 1997 on PlayStation and later on the Sega Saturn. The SNES version is an unofficial fan port.

### Where can I download the Symphony of the Night SNES demo?

For free from developer Kaffeeware's itch.io page. It works on emulators and on real SNES consoles with a flash cartridge.

### Will the SNES port be complete?

No. The author has said they do not intend to port the whole game, and in particular will leave out the inverted castle.
